Her grief at his condition and the sight of her doing his hard work moved him more deeply than a lecture. He stopped drinking and eventually became a notary. ସୁଯୋଗ ଓ ମନପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ ଉଭୟ ଦରକାର ହେଲା।

“Changed him” ଅର୍ଥ ଜୀବିକା ଓ ମନୋଭାବ ଉଭୟ ବଦଳିବା। କାମର ସୁଯୋଗ ଜୀବିକାକୁ ସହାୟ ହେଲା।

ଗୋପନରେ ତାଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ କଷ୍ଟ କରିଥିବା ମଣିଷକୁ ଦେଖିବା ନିଜ ଆଚରଣ ବଦଳାଇବାର ପ୍ରେରଣା ଦେଲା।

D. 6Which character do you prefer, and why?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

I most admire Olga. She did not approve of the beggar’s conduct, but she saw that his weakness was real. Without seeking praise she did a difficult task on his behalf. Her kindness helped him change his own way of life. Lushkoff’s later account reveals how much her actions meant to him. ଅନ୍ୟ ପାତ୍ର ନେଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ କାରଣ ଦିଅ।

ନିଜ ମତକୁ “I admire … because …” ଆକାରରେ ଆରମ୍ଭ କରି ପ୍ରମାଣ ଯୋଡ଼। ଯଦି Sergeiଙ୍କୁ ବାଛ, ପରିଚୟପତ୍ର ଦେବା ତାଙ୍କ ସଂଶୋଧିତ ସହାୟତାର ଏକ ଭଲ ଉଦାହରଣ।

D. 7Suggest ways of helping people who have to beg.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

People who beg may need immediate food as well as a dependable livelihood. Help is most effective when it takes account of a person’s condition and abilities. Sergei eventually finds work suited to Lushkoff’s skill in writing. Olga shows the importance of personal kindness. Practical opportunities and respectful treatment can work together. ଏହା ମତଭିତ୍ତିକ ଉତ୍ତର।

ତୁରନ୍ତ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ ଓ ଦୀର୍ଘକାଳୀନ କାମ ଅଲଗା ଆବଶ୍ୟକତା ପୂରଣ କରନ୍ତି। ଦୁର୍ବଳ ଲୋକ ପାଇଁ କାଠ ଫାଡ଼ିବା ଅପେକ୍ଷା copying ସମ୍ଭବ ଥିଲା।
