୧. How can two sentences be simple but have different purposes?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

A statement and a question may each contain one independent clause. The former conveys information; the latter asks for it. Clause structure and the speaker’s purpose must therefore be examined separately. ଗଠନ ଓ ଉଦ୍ଦେଶ୍ୟ ଦୁଇଟି ଅଲଗା ପରୀକ୍ଷା।
