A · Lead-in. Discuss the opening questions about air and use the examples to predict the topic.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

A pupil might say, “Smoke on a busy road makes me cough.” Another may never have had that experience. The questions prepare us to connect what we breathe with health and to ask what makes rainfall acidic. ନିଜ ଅନୁଭବ ଥିଲେ ଲେଖ; ପାଠର ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ କୌଣସି ପଦାର୍ଥ ଚାଖି ଦେଖିବାକୁ କହୁନାହିଁ।

D. 1–5. Explain the opening example and the importance of inhaled air.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. Gas escaped from a chemical plant at Bhopal in 1984. 2. The escape occurred at an industrial plant. 3. Many people died and others were injured or lost their eyesight. 4. Breathing is essential for life. 5. Polluted air is an important route by which contaminants enter the body.

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବୁଝାମଣା — ନିଃଶ୍ୱାସ ପାଇଁ ଦରକାରୀ ବାୟୁ ଦିନଭରି ଶରୀରକୁ ପ୍ରବେଶ କରେ। ପାଠର ଭୋପାଳ ଘଟଣା ଏହି ମାଧ୍ୟମରେ ବିଷାକ୍ତ ଗ୍ୟାସର ବିପଦ ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

Bhopal ଥିଲା ହଠାତ୍ ବିଷାକ୍ତ ଗ୍ୟାସ୍ ନିଷ୍କାସନର ଉଦାହରଣ। ଆମେ ନିରନ୍ତର ଶ୍ୱାସ ନେଉଥିବାରୁ ଜଳ ବା ଖାଦ୍ୟ ପରି ସହଜରେ ବାୟୁକୁ ଏଡ଼ିପାରୁନାହୁଁ।

ଏହା ଆଗର ଘଟଣାକୁ ସମସ୍ତଙ୍କ ସମସ୍ୟା କରେ।

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

6. The text gives 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, a little under 1% argon and 0.03% carbon dioxide. 7. A change such as a lower oxygen level or the entry of irritating gases makes air polluted. ଶତକଡ଼ା ସଂଖ୍ୟା ଏହି ପାଠରେ ଦିଆଯାଇଥିବା ମୂଲ୍ୟ।

ଏକା ସୂଚୀକୁ ଦୁଇଥର ଦେଲେ ତାହା ବୁଝାଯାଏନାହିଁ।

D. 8–13. Connect industry and traffic with emissions.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

8. Coal, coke and furnace oil are named. 9. Dust, ash and gases are inhaled. 10. Thermal stations release large quantities of smoke and ash. 11. Cement, steel, ore-processing and chemical industries also contribute. 12. A growing population needs transport. 13. Vehicle exhaust adds harmful gases, including carbon monoxide.

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବୁଝାମଣା — ଶିଳ୍ପରେ ଇନ୍ଧନ ଦହନରୁ ଧୂଆଁ, ଯାନବାହନରୁ ନିର୍ଗତ ଗ୍ୟାସ; ଏ ଦୁଇ ଉତ୍ସର ଫରକ ମନେ ରଖ।

Coal ଓ oil ଜାଳେଣି, ash ଓ exhaust ନିଷ୍କାସିତ ପଦାର୍ଥ। ତାପବିଦ୍ୟୁତ କେନ୍ଦ୍ର ଏକ ସ୍ଥିର ଉତ୍ସ; ମୋଟରଗାଡ଼ି ସହର ଭିତରେ ଚଳନ୍ତା ଉତ୍ସ।

କେଉଁ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନରେ ଜାଳେଣି, କେଉଁଥିରେ କ୍ଷତି ମାଗୁଛି ଅଲଗା କର।

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

14. Vehicle exhaust harms flower and vegetable crops, and pollution from power plants has killed trees. 15. Soot blackens walls and polluted air damages building surfaces. 16. D. 17–18. Distinguish the warming effect from the harm due to refrigerants.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

17. More carbon dioxide, methane and nitrous oxide can trap radiation and warm the atmosphere. 18. The lesson describes chlorofluorocarbons used in refrigeration as a threat to the layer that protects us from harmful solar energy. ତାପମାତ୍ରା ବଢ଼ିବା ଓ ସୁରକ୍ଷାଦାୟୀ ସ୍ତର ନଷ୍ଟ ହେବା ଦୁଇଟି ଭିନ୍ନ ପ୍ରଭାବ।

D. 19–22. Explain acid rain, its effects and the three controls.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

19. The passage links acidic precipitation to sulphur and nitrogen compounds. 20. Rivers and lakes become acidic, harming aquatic life. 21. Useful soil nutrients are washed away. 22. Use preventive, dispersal and collection measures.

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବୁଝାମଣା — ବର୍ଷା ପାଣିର ପ୍ରଭାବ ଓ ମାଟିର ପୋଷକ ଦ୍ରବ୍ୟ ହ୍ରାସ ଅଲଗା ଭାବେ ଲେଖ। ତିନି ନିୟନ୍ତ୍ରଣ ଉପାୟରୁ ଗୋଟିଏ ଲେଖାଏଁ ଉଦାହରଣ ବାଛ।

Acid rainରେ ପ୍ରଥମେ ବାୟୁର ଦୂଷକ, ପରେ ଅମ୍ଳୀୟ ବର୍ଷା, ଶେଷରେ ଜଳ ଓ ମାଟିର କ୍ଷତି। ନିୟନ୍ତ୍ରଣରେ ଏହି କ୍ରମକୁ ଓଲଟାଇ ଉତ୍ସରେ କମାଇବା ବା ବାହାରିବା ପଥରେ ଧରିବା ଭାବ।

D. 23–25. Why do standards, technology and research all matter?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

23. Countries pass laws and set air-quality standards. 24. Low-sulphur fuels and redesigned engines have reduced pollution. 25. Pollutants cross boundaries and effective control remains costly, so better ways are needed.

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବୁଝାମଣା — ବିଧି ପ୍ରଣୟନ ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟ ସ୍ଥିର କରେ। ଇନ୍ଧନ ଓ ଇଞ୍ଜିନର ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ ବାସ୍ତବରେ ନିର୍ଗମନ ହ୍ରାସ କରେ।

Standard ବାୟୁର ଗୁଣ ମାପିବା ପାଇଁ ମାନ ଦିଏ। ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତିତ engine ଓ fuel ସେହି ମାନକୁ ପହଞ୍ଚିବାର ଉପାୟ।

Research ପରର ଉପାୟ ଖୋଜେ। ଆଇନ କଲାମାତ୍ରେ ଦୂଷକ ଆପେଆପେ କମେନାହିଁ।

E (i). 1–3. Explain changes to the atmosphere, the risk from a chemical plant and the formation of acid rain.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. Burning fuels uses oxygen and releases other substances. The lesson warns that altered air can lead to respiratory disorders. 2. Chemical plants may release toxic fumes. A gas escape can kill or injure many people, as at Bhopal. 3. Sulphur and nitrogen compounds enter the air; rain or other precipitation containing these pollutants falls as acid rain. ପ୍ରତି ଉତ୍ତରରେ ପ୍ରଥମେ କାରଣ ଓ ପରେ ପରିଣାମକୁ ରଖ।

E (ii). Fill the cause, affected area and damage table with examples from the lesson.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Industry → air → smoke and ash; cars → crops → exhaust damage; acid rain → soil → loss of calcium, potassium and magnesium; refrigerants containing chlorofluorocarbons → atmosphere → damage to its protective layer. ପ୍ରତି ଧାଡ଼ିରେ ଉତ୍ସ, କେଉଁଠାରେ ପ୍ରଭାବ ପଡ଼େ ଓ କି ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ ହୁଏ ଦେଖାଅ।
