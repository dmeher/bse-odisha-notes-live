A. 1–4 · Before you read. Compare a familiar grain with the title and plan the family discussion.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. A single grain of rice or wheat is much smaller than an egg. 2. Ask a relative what they remember and record their own reply. 3. An egg-sized grain could belong to an older generation in a tale. The story later offers a moral explanation for the change. 4. A group note could distinguish observation, a relative’s recollection and an imagined answer. ନମୁନା ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ: Were the grains you grew larger than the ones you see today?

ପରିବାରର ଉତ୍ତର ସେମାନଙ୍କଠାରୁ ହିଁ ନିଅ।

ଆଜିର ଅନୁଭବକୁ “I have seen …” ଓ ଗଳ୍ପ ଆରମ୍ଭ ପୂର୍ବର ଅନୁମାନକୁ “I think …” କହି ଅଲଗା ରଖ। ଶେଷରେ ଜେଜେବାପାଙ୍କ ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା ସହ ଅନୁମାନ ମିଳାଇଲେ ଆଲୋଚନାର ଉତ୍ତର ବଦଳିପାରେ।

Comprehension check I. 1–4. Explain the discovery and the limits of the wise men’s knowledge.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. The children found a grain-shaped object as large as a hen’s egg. 2. A hen pecked into it and exposed its nature. 3. The king asked his wise men and then summoned old peasants. 4. The scholars could not find its history in books and suggested asking peasants. ବସ୍ତୁଟି କ’ଣ ଓ କେବେ ଉତ୍ପାଦିତ ହୋଇଥିଲା—ଏହି ଦୁଇ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନର ଉତ୍ତର ଭିନ୍ନ ଉପାୟରେ ମିଳିଲା।

ଆକାରଟି ପିଲାଙ୍କୁ ଆଶ୍ଚର୍ଯ୍ୟ କରେ, ଥଣ୍ଟର ଆଘାତ ଜିନିଷର ପ୍ରକୃତି ଦେଖାଏ। ଶସ୍ୟ କେବେ ଓ କେଉଁଠି ଫଳିଥିଲା ବୋଲି ରାଜାଙ୍କ ପର ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ଏକ ଜୀବନ୍ତ ଇତିହାସ ଚାହେ।

ସେଥିପାଇଁ ପଣ୍ଡିତମାନେ ଚାଷୀଙ୍କ କଥା କହିଲେ।

Comprehension check I. 5–8. What did the first two peasants know about the grain?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

5. The king asked where such corn grew and whether the first peasant had bought or sown it. 6. His sight and hearing were poor, and he remembered no such corn. 7. His father used one crutch and could see better. 8. He remembered larger grain than people grew later but could not recognise this specimen. ପ୍ରଥମ ଚାଷୀଙ୍କ ବାପା ନିଜ ଅନୁଭବର ସୀମା କହି ତାଙ୍କ ବାପାଙ୍କୁ ପଚାରିବାକୁ କହିଲେ।

ପ୍ରଥମ ଲୋକ ଛୋଟ ଦାନା କିଣନ୍ତି। ବାପା ନିଜେ ଚାଷ କରୁଥିବାରୁ ନିଜ ସମୟର ଫସଲ ସହ ତୁଳନା କରିପାରନ୍ତି।

ତଥାପି ନମୁନାଟି ସେ ଦେଖିଥିବା ଶସ୍ୟଠାରୁ ବଡ଼। ଏହି ସୀମା ସ୍ୱୀକାର କରି ସେ ନିଜ ବାପାଙ୍କ କଥା କହନ୍ତି।

Comprehension check II. 1–5. Compare the older generations and identify the source of the grain.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. The father’s corn was bigger than the present corn but smaller than the specimen. 2. The grandfather walked easily, saw clearly and heard well. 3. He recognised corn he had grown and eaten in his youth. 4. People grew their own food and did not buy or sell bread. 5. Land was open to those who ploughed it. ପିଢ଼ି ଯେତେ ପୁରୁଣା, ସ୍ମୃତି ତେତେ ସେହି ଦାନାର ସମୟ ନିକଟର।

ଜେଜେବାପାଙ୍କ ଉତ୍ତର “I grew it” ପ୍ରକାରର ପ୍ରତ୍ୟକ୍ଷ ସାକ୍ଷ୍ୟ। ସେ ସମୟରେ ଲୋକ ନିଜେ ଚାଷ କରି ଖାଉଥିଲେ।

ତେଣୁ ରାଜା ପଚାରିଥିବା ଦାମ ଓ କିଣା ଜମି ସେହି ପ୍ରଥାକୁ ବୁଝାଇବାରେ ଲାଗେନାହିଁ।

Comprehension check II. 6–7. What final comparisons did the king wish to explain?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

6. He asked why grain had become smaller and why the oldest man was fitter than his descendants. 7. The grandfather spoke of living by one’s own labour, sharing and not coveting another person’s produce. ଗଳ୍ପ ନୈତିକ ଶିକ୍ଷା ଦେବାକୁ ଏହି ତୁଳନା କରିଛି।

ରାଜାଙ୍କ ଗୋଟିଏ ତୁଳନା ଶସ୍ୟର, ଅନ୍ୟଟି ଶରୀରର। ଜେଜେବାପା ଉଭୟକୁ ଏକ ଜୀବନନୀତି ସହ ଯୋଡ଼ନ୍ତି।

“In the grandfather’s view” ଲେଖିଲେ ଏହା ଗଳ୍ପର ପାତ୍ରର ମତ ତାହା ସ୍ପଷ୍ଟ ହୁଏ।

D. 1. How did the grain reach the king?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Children found an unusual object in a ravine. It had the shape and groove of a grain but was as large as a hen’s egg. A traveller bought it from them for a penny. He then carried it into town and sold it to the king as a curiosity. ଘଟଣାରେ ବିନିମୟର ଦୁଇ ପଦକ୍ଷେପ ଅଛି।

ଲେଖିବାବେଳେ subject ବଦଳିବା ଧ୍ୟାନ କର। Children found it, a traveller bought it, the king received it. ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ କ୍ରିୟାର କର୍ତ୍ତା ଭିନ୍ନ, ତେଣୁ ଯାତ୍ରୀ ନିଜେ ଦାନା ଖୋଜିଥିଲେ ବୋଲି ଭୁଲ୍ ହେବନାହିଁ।

D. 2. How was the grain identified?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

The king’s wise men examined the strange object but could not identify it at first. While it was lying on a windowsill, a hen flew in and pecked a hole in it. The opening revealed its nature. The men could now recognise it as a grain of corn and tell the king. ପରିଚୟ ମିଳିବାରେ କୁକୁଡ଼ାର ଭୂମିକା ଅଛି।

ଖାଜ ଥିବା ଦେଖି ପ୍ରଥମେ ଶସ୍ୟ ବୋଲି ଅନୁମାନ କରାଯାଇପାରେ। ଭିତର ଦେଖିବାର ସୁଯୋଗ ନିଶ୍ଚୟତା ଦିଏ।

କୁକୁଡ଼ାର ଆକସ୍ମିକ କାମ କାହିଁକି ଗଳ୍ପକୁ ଆଗକୁ ନିଏ ଏହି ଫରକରୁ ବୁଝାପଡ଼େ।

D. 3. Why did the learned men recommend consulting peasants?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Learning what the object was did not explain when and where corn of that size had grown. The wise men searched their books without discovering an answer. They therefore suggested consulting elderly peasants. People who worked the land might remember such grain or have heard about it from their fathers. ଲିଖିତ ତଥ୍ୟ ନ ମିଳିବାରୁ ମୌଖିକ ସ୍ମୃତି ଖୋଜାଗଲା।

ପୁସ୍ତକରେ ଉତ୍ତର ନ ମିଳିବାକୁ ପଣ୍ଡିତଙ୍କ ଅଳସୁଆପଣ ବୋଲି ଲେଖିବାର ଆଧାର ନାହିଁ। ଦାନା ଚିହ୍ନଟ ପରେ ତାହାର ଅତୀତ ସମ୍ପର୍କରେ ସେମାନଙ୍କ ଜ୍ଞାନ ସରିଗଲା।

ଦୀର୍ଘ ଅଭିଜ୍ଞତା ଥିବା ଚାଷୀଙ୍କ ସ୍ମୃତି ହିଁ ପରର ସମ୍ଭାବ୍ୟ ସୂତ୍ର।

D. 4. Compare the replies of the first old peasant and his father.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

The first peasant said he had neither sown nor harvested such corn, and the grains he bought were small. His father had grown his own corn instead of buying it. His grain was bigger than present-day corn, but never as large as the specimen. He advised the king to ask his father. ଦାନା କିଣିବା ଓ ନିଜେ ଉତ୍ପାଦନ କରିବାର ଫରକ ଦିଅ।

ଉତ୍ତରକୁ ଦୁଇ ଅକ୍ଷରେ ସଜାଇହୁଏ। ଗୋଟିଏରେ bought ଓ grew, ଅନ୍ୟଟିରେ small ଓ bigger than our present grain ରହିବ।

ତଥାପି ଦୁଇଜଣଙ୍କ ମଧ୍ୟରୁ କେହି ସେହି ଅତିବଡ଼ ଦାନାକୁ ନିଜ ସମୟର ବୋଲି କହିନାହାନ୍ତି।

D. 5. Describe the three old men in the order of their appearance.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

The first peasant was bent, pale, toothless and dependent on two crutches. He could hardly see or hear. His father managed with one crutch and could see and hear better. The grandfather, the eldest of the three, walked without support. His sight and hearing were clear and he spoke distinctly. ଦୁଇ, ଏକ ଓ ଶୂନ୍ୟ ଆଶାବାଡ଼ି ତିନି ପିଢ଼ିର ସ୍ୱାସ୍ଥ୍ୟ ସୂଚାଏ।

ଦୁଇଟି, ଗୋଟିଏ, କିଛି ନାହିଁ—ଆଶାବାଡ଼ିର ଏହି କ୍ରମ ସ୍ମରଣରେ ରଖ। ବୟସ ବଢ଼ୁଥିବା କ୍ରମରେ ଭରସାର ଆବଶ୍ୟକତା କମୁଛି।

ଏହି ଓଲଟା ଚିତ୍ର ରାଜାଙ୍କ ଶେଷ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନକୁ ପ୍ରସ୍ତୁତ କରେ।

D. 6. What sort of life did the grandfather remember?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

The grandfather described a time when land was freely available. A person ploughed the ground and lived by the labour of his own hands. Corn was plentiful enough to feed oneself and others. People had no use for money to buy bread and did not covet the produce of another person’s work. ଶ୍ରମ, ଜମି ଓ ବିନିମୟ ତିନୋଟି ଦିଗ ଅଛି।

ନିଜ ହାତର ଶ୍ରମ ମଣିଷକୁ ଖାଦ୍ୟ ଦେଉଥିଲା। ଜମି ଅବାଧ ଓ ଶସ୍ୟ ପ୍ରଚୁର ଥିବାରୁ ଅନ୍ୟକୁ ଦେବା ସମ୍ଭବ ଥିଲା।

ଉତ୍ତରରେ ଏହି ଯୋଗଟି ଲେଖିଲେ ଜମି, ରୁଟି ଓ ଟଙ୍କାର ବିବରଣୀ ଏକାଠି ଅର୍ଥ ଦିଏ।

D. 7. How did the grandfather explain the change in later generations?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

In the grandfather’s view, people had turned from the rule by which earlier generations lived. They desired what others produced and relied on other people’s labour. He offered that change as his moral explanation both for the poorer grain and for the weakness of his younger descendants. These are the character’s conclusions in the story. ଏହା କାହାଣୀର ନୈତିକ ବ୍ୟାଖ୍ୟା।

‘Covet’ ଅର୍ଥ ଅନ୍ୟର ଜିନିଷ ନିଜର କରିବାକୁ ଲୋଭ କରିବା। ଜେଜେବାପା ନିଜ ଶ୍ରମରେ ଜୀବନ ଚଳାଇବା ସହ ଏହାକୁ ତୁଳନା କରନ୍ତି।

ତାଙ୍କ ବିଚାରରେ ଆଚରଣ ବଦଳିବା ଫସଲ ଓ ଶରୀରର ଅବନତିର ଏକ କାରଣ।

E. Activity 1 (a), (b). Complete the family tree and describe the three men in order of appearance.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Family tree from eldest to youngest: grandfather → his son (the second visitor) → his grandson (the first visitor). Word boxes in story order: first visitor — toothless, old and bent, ashy pale, two crutches; second — one crutch, still able to see, heard better; third — without crutches, eyes clear, hearing good, spoke distinctly. Sentences: The first visitor leaned on two crutches and struggled to see. His father walked with one and could still inspect the corn.
