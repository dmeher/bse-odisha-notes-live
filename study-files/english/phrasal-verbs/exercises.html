Activity 2 (1–5). Replace the five objects with pronouns in order.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. Can you pick them out? 2. We have pointed them out. 3. It is time to wind it up. 4. The soldiers pulled it down. 5. Can you carry it on a little longer?

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବୁଝାମଣା — Separable verbର ମଝିରେ object pronoun ବସିବ। ବହୁବଚନ ପାଇଁ them ଓ ଏକବଚନ ପାଇଁ it।

Pick out ଓ point outରେ them plural object; wind up ଓ pull downରେ it singular object। ଏଗୁଡ଼ିକ separable ହେତୁ ଉଭୟ pronoun verb ଓ particle ମଝିରେ ରହେ।

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

6. threw them away; 7. closed it down; 8. lay it by; 9. look it up; 10. ruled it out.

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବୁଝାମଣା — Look it upରେ it ମଝିରେ। ଅର୍ଥ ସହିତ particleର ସ୍ଥାନ ମନେ ରଖ।

Throw them awayରେ them ଏକାଧିକ ବସ୍ତୁ ପାଇଁ, close it downରେ it ଗୋଟିଏ ସଂସ୍ଥା ପାଇଁ। Look it up ମାନେ ଖୋଜି ଦେଖିବା, rule it out ମାନେ ସମ୍ଭାବନାକୁ ବାଦ୍ ଦେବା।

Activity 3 (1–5). Supply the correct forms and object positions.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. came across a strange animal; 2. throw away broken chairs; 3. pointed it out; 4. looking after this building; 5. got over his sorrows.

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବୁଝାମଣା — Look after ଅବିଭାଜ୍ୟ; point outରେ itକୁ ମଝିରେ ରଖିବାକୁ ହୁଏ।

Came across ଅର୍ଥ ହଠାତ୍ ଦେଖିଲା, looked after ଯତ୍ନ ନେଲା, got over ପାର ହେଲା/ସୁସ୍ଥ ହେଲା। ଏଗୁଡ଼ିକର object ପରେ ରହେ।

Pointed it outରେ object pronoun ମଝିରେ, pointed past form।

Activity 3. 6–10. Put the pronoun and particle in the appropriate order and supply the tense.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

6 switch it off; 7 turned it down; 8 was asking after you; 9 close it down; 10 stand by him. ୯ର ଛପା ବାକ୍ୟରେ will ଅଛି। ଖାଲି ସ୍ଥାନର ବ୍ୟାକରଣରୂପ close it down ହେଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ ସ୍କୁଲ ଭଲ ଚାଲିଛି ବୋଲି ପୂର୍ବ କଥା ସହ ମେଳ ପାଇଁ will not close it down ଅଧିକ ସ୍ୱାଭାବିକ।

Switch it off, turn it down ଓ close it downର pronoun ମଝିରେ। Ask after you ଖବର ପଚାରିବା ଓ stand by him ସାହାଯ୍ୟରେ ରହିବାରେ pronoun ଶେଷରେ।

Will ପରେ close, closed ନୁହେଁ।

Activity 4 (i–xi). Choose the particle or particles after look from the meaning supplied.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

i out; ii after; iii into; iv upon (or on); v up; vi down upon (or down on); vii forward to; viii at; ix on; x up to; xi for. Look on ଦେଖି ରହିବା, look up to ସମ୍ମାନ କରିବା ଓ look down on ତୁଚ୍ଛ ମଣିବା।

ଶେଷରେ ଦିଆଯାଇଥିବା ଦଳୀୟ ଖେଳ ପାଇଁ ଉଦାହରଣ: give up hope; put on a coat.

Look after ଯତ୍ନ, look into ତଦନ୍ତ, look up ଖୋଜିବା, look down on ଅବଜ୍ଞା, look forward to ଆଶାରେ ଅପେକ୍ଷା, look up to ସମ୍ମାନ, look for ସନ୍ଧାନ। ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକରେ ପର ଶବ୍ଦ ବା ଦୁଇ ଶବ୍ଦ ବଦଳିଲେ ଅର୍ଥ ବଦଳେ।

Activity 5 (i–xv). Select and inflect the verb before up.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

i make; ii pack; iii set; iv used; v broke; vi get; vii looking; viii turned; ix blew; x bring; xi did; xii fixed; xiii give; xiv held; xv keep. Have ପରେ used ଓ fixed; is ପରେ looking।

Did up = decorated, held up = delayed. ଖେଳ ପାଇଁ ଅନ୍ୟ ଉଦାହରଣ: turn off the radio; look after the child.

Up ସମସ୍ତ ଖାଲି ଜାଗାରେ ଦିଆଥିବାରୁ କାମ ଚିହ୍ନାଇବ ମୂଳ verb। Set up ସ୍ଥାପନା, use up ଶେଷ କରିବା, turn up ଉପସ୍ଥିତ ହେବା, give up ଛାଡ଼ିବା।

Past ଦରକାର ହେଲେ break → broke, blow → blew, do → did।

After Activity 4 · Group competition. Use each of the other five suggested verbs with a particle and a meaning clue.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

break down: The bus stopped working. come across: I found an old letter by chance. give up: She stopped making excuses. go on: He continued reading. put out: The caretaker extinguished the light. One partner reads the clue and the other supplies the expression. ଉତ୍ତର ଯାଞ୍ଚ ପାଇଁ ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ବାକ୍ୟରେ phrasal verb ବସାଅ।

Clue ଓ ଉତ୍ତରକୁ ଓଲଟା ପରଖ। “A bus stopped working”ରେ broke down ବସାଇଲେ ଅର୍ଥ ଫେରେ; “I met it by chance”ରେ came across।

Break ବା come ଏକା ବସାଇଲେ ଏହି ସୂଚନା ମିଳେନାହିଁ।

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

i is being brought up; ii turned down; iii was called off; iv cut down (or cut down on); v let my parents down; vi make out; vii take after; viii sent for; ix kept on working; x gone through. କାଳ ଓ voice ରଖ।

After Activity 5 · Group competition. Use each of the five additional particles to make a clue.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Particle ଦେଖି ବାସ୍ତବ କାମର ଛୋଟ clue ଦିଅ। Look for a key ମାନେ ଚାବି ହଜିଥିଲେ ଖୋଜିବା, look after a child ମାନେ ଶିଶୁର ଯତ୍ନ।

ଏଭଳି ଅଲଗା clue ଥିଲେ ଦଳ ଠିକ୍ particle ବାଛିପାରେ।
