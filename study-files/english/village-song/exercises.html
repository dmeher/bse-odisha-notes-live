A–B · Listening. How can two readers distinguish the speakers and the daughter’s choice?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

The mother’s repeated address should sound tender and questioning. The daughter’s answer may grow firmer as she listens to the imagined forest. Read the alternating stanzas separately, then mark a word of sound, such as calling, and one of colour, such as saffron. କବିତାର ଦୁଇ ସ୍ୱରକୁ ପଢ଼ାରେ ଅଲଗା କର।

ମା’ଙ୍କ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ଉଚ୍ଚାରଣରେ ବିରାମ ଦିଅ। ଝିଅର ଜବାବରେ ବନର soundsକୁ ଜୋର ଦେଲେ ଦୁଇ କଣ୍ଠସ୍ୱରର ଭିନ୍ନ ଆକର୍ଷଣ ଶୁଣାଯିବ।

F. 1–4. Identify the speakers, destination, motive and forest details.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. A mother and her daughter. 2. The wild forest. 3. She feels drawn by the fairy folk and wants to remain a child. 4. Champa buds open, lilies gleam and the koil haunts the river-isles.

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବୁଝାମଣା — କିଏ କାହାକୁ କହୁଛି, ସେ କେଉଁଠି ଯିବାକୁ ଚାହେଁ ଓ ସେଠାରେ କ’ଣ ଦେଖେ—ଏହି କ୍ରମରେ ଉତ୍ତର ଗଢ଼ନ୍ତୁ।

Destination ଜଙ୍ଗଲ, motive ହେଉଛି ତାକୁ ଟାଣୁଥିବା ଗୀତ ଓ ସେଠି ରହିବାର ଇଚ୍ଛା। Champa ଓ koilର ଚିତ୍ର ଏହି ଡାକକୁ କେବଳ ‘nature’ ନାମ ଅପେକ୍ଷା ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ କରେ।

F. 5–8. Compare the two kinds of music and their meaning.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

5. The mother means the joys of marriage, children and leisure. 6. Both songs are part of family life and both sound sad to the girl. 7. Today’s laughter is set against tomorrow’s death. 8. For the daughter the music of streams is sweeter than the songs of domestic life.

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବୁଝାମଣା — ଏକେ ପାରିବାରିକ ସଙ୍ଗୀତ ମାଆଙ୍କୁ ସୁଖ ଓ ଝିଅକୁ ଦୁଃଖର ସମ୍ଭାବନା ଶୁଣାଏ।

Wedding ଓ cradleର ଗୀତରେ ମା’ଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ ଜୀବନର ଆନନ୍ଦ ଅଛି। ଝିଅର କାନ ସେଥିର ଅସ୍ଥାୟିତ୍ୱ ମଧ୍ୟ ଶୁଣେ।

Forest song କାହିଁକି ତାକୁ ବେଶି ଡାକେ ଏହି ଫରକରୁ ବୁଝାଯାଏ।

H. 1–3. Explain the mother’s opening appeal and contrast the attractions.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. Her four questions remind the girl of her jewels, her mother’s care and the waiting lover. 2. The girl remains drawn to nature and the fairies. 3. Domestic joy has a shadow of sorrow for the girl; the forest’s music seems sweeter. ମାଆ ଓ ଝିଅ ଭିନ୍ନ ଆନନ୍ଦକୁ ମୂଲ୍ୟ ଦେଉଛନ୍ତି।

ମା’ଙ୍କ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନଗୁଡ଼ିକ ସାଧାରଣ ଖବର ମାଗୁନାହିଁ। ସେ ସ୍ନେହ, ଅଳଙ୍କାର ଓ ଅପେକ୍ଷାରତ ବର ମନେ ପକାଇ ଝିଅକୁ ରୋକିବାକୁ ଚାହୁଁଛନ୍ତି।

ତେଣୁ ଉତ୍ତରରେ appeal ଶବ୍ଦ ଖାପ ଖାଏ।

I. 1–3. Count would and the questions; suggest a further bridal expression.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. “Would” occurs three times. 2. Five questions appear in the poem: four in stanza 1 and one in stanza 3. The mother asks them all. 3. Sample: bridal jewellery. ଶବ୍ଦ ଗଣିବାବେଳେ lead-in ନୁହେଁ, କେବଳ ମୂଳ କବିତା ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ।

Would ଥିବା ଧାଡ଼ି ଓ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନଚିହ୍ନର ଧାଡ଼ି ସବୁବେଳେ ଏକେ ନୁହେଁ। ଦୁଇ ଗଣନା ପୃଥକ କର।

ନିଜ bridal expression ଦେବା ବେଳେ ସେହିଟି ମୂଳ କବିତାର ଉଦ୍ଧୃତି ନୁହେଁ ବୋଲି ଜଣାଅ।

I. 4–6. Use who, order the images and identify the sound pairs.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

4. The boy who waters the tree waits by the gate. The singer who knows the tune will lead. 5. Champa buds → koil-haunted river-isles → lotus lilies → bridal robes → bridal cakes. 6. Stanza 1: going/blowing, fed you/wed you; 2: going/blowing, glisten/listen; 3: pleasure/leisure, glowing/going; 4: sorrow/to-morrow, falling/calling. The first two repeat going/blowing, while going returns in stanza 3. The -ing endings help the exchange sound like a continuing song. ପ୍ରତି ସ୍ତବକର ଦୁଇ ଯୋଡ଼ା ଶେଷ ଧ୍ୱନିକୁ ସ୍ୱରରେ ପଢ଼।

Who ପୂର୍ବରେ person ଓ ପରର clauseରେ ସେ କରୁଥିବା କାମ ରଖ। Sound pairs ପାଇଁ ଶେଷ syllable ଶୁଣ; ଚିତ୍ର ସଜାଣିରେ ଝିଅ କେଉଁ କ୍ରମରେ ଦୃଶ୍ୟ ଡାକୁଛି ଦେଖ।

J · Speaking. Suggest a contribution to the group discussion about mothers.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Sample: We can listen when our mothers are worried, share work at home and thank them for their care. Each group can give one concrete example. ଏହା ଖୋଲା ଆଲୋଚନା; ନିଜ ଅନୁଭବ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରନ୍ତୁ।

ସମୂହର ପରବର୍ତ୍ତୀ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ହୋଇପାରେ “How can we show we have listened?” ନମୁନା ଉତ୍ତର “We can ask what worries her and offer to share one task.” ଏହା ସମ୍ମାନକୁ ଏକ କାମରେ ଦେଖାଏ।

K. 1–4. Write about the mother, the destination, the two groups of images and fairyland.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. The mother appeals to affection and describes wedding preparations. 2. The girl hears the fairy folk and the streams. 3. Home: jewels, robes, cakes and cradle-songs. Nature: champa, lilies, koil and streams. 4. The imagined forest is bright with opening flowers and alive with music; to the girl its voices are an invitation. ଶେଷ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନରେ କବିତାରେ ଥିବା ଦୃଶ୍ୟ ଓ ଶ୍ରାବ୍ୟ ଚିତ୍ର ଏକାଠି ଲେଖନ୍ତୁ।

Mother ଅଂଶରେ ଘର ଓ ବିବାହର ଚିତ୍ର, daughter ଅଂଶରେ ବନ ଓ ସଙ୍ଗୀତ। Fairyland ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା ନିଜ କଳ୍ପନା ହେଲେ କବିତାରୁ ମିଳିଥିବା ଫୁଲ, ପକ୍ଷୀ ବା ନଦୀକୂଳକୁ ଆଧାର କର।
