D. 1. Introduce Sergei and Lushkoff and describe the first encounter in the story.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Sergei was an advocate. Lushkoff was a former choir singer who approached him as a poor teacher who had lost his position. The worn coat and unequal overshoes caught Sergei’s attention. Recognising the man who had previously claimed to be a student, Sergei challenged him to explain his contradictory stories. ଦୁଇ ପରିଚୟ ମଧ୍ୟରେ ବିରୋଧ ତାଙ୍କ ମିଥ୍ୟାକୁ ଧରାଇଲା।

Teacher ପରିଚୟଟି Lushkoffଙ୍କ ସେତେବେଳର ଦାବି। Former choir singer ହେଉଛି ପରେ ସ୍ୱୀକାର କରିଥିବା ପରିଚୟ।

“He claimed to be …” କହିଲେ ଦୁଇଟି ମିଶିବ ନାହିଁ।

D. 2. Explain the circumstances and choices that led Lushkoff to beg and lie.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Lushkoff was poor and unable to find a secure livelihood, but he also admitted that his drinking had cost him his place in a choir. He chose to invent respectable stories about teaching and studying. He told Sergei that nobody gave him anything when he spoke honestly about his past. ପରିସ୍ଥିତି ଓ ନିଜ ପସନ୍ଦ ଦୁଇଟି ଦିଗ ଅଛି।

ତାଙ୍କ ଅସହାୟତା ଓ ନିଜ ପସନ୍ଦ ଉଭୟ ରଖ। କେବଳ “he was poor” କହିଲେ ମଦ ପାଇଁ କାମ ହରାଇବା ଓ ମିଛ ପରିଚୟ ଦେବାର ତାଙ୍କ ନିଜ ସ୍ୱୀକାରୋକ୍ତି ହଜିଯାଏ।

D. 3. Explain why he accepted work and what tasks he later undertook.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

After Sergei challenged him, Lushkoff felt too ashamed and proud to refuse the offer to chop wood. He followed Olga although he was weak. He came back on later occasions for work such as clearing snow, tidying the shed and beating dust out of rugs. He also helped when Sergei moved house. କାମ କରିବାକୁ ରାଜି ହେବା ଓ କିଏ କାଠ କାଟିଲା ଅଲଗା।

ପ୍ରଥମେ ସମ୍ମତିର କାରଣ, ତା’ପରେ ଘରକୁ ପୁଣିପୁଣି ଆସିବା ଅଲଗା ରଖ। ପାରିଶ୍ରମିକ ପାଇଲେ ବୋଲି ସେ ନିଜେ ସବୁ କାଠ ଫାଡ଼ିଲା ବୋଲି ଲେଖିଲେ ଶେଷ ଖୋଲାସା ସହ ମେଳିବନାହିଁ।

D. 4. Write a short character portrait of Olga.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Olga was Sergei’s cook. Her angry words made her seem harsh when she first led Lushkoff to the woodshed. She saw his weakness, however, and chopped the wood for him herself. Her tears showed genuine distress. Because her actions matched her concern, Lushkoff remembered her kindness long after leaving the house. କଥା ଏବଂ ବ୍ୟବହାରର ଫରକକୁ ଦେଖାଅ।

ତାଙ୍କ କଥା ମାତ୍ର ପଢ଼ିଲେ କଠୋର ଲାଗେ। କାନ୍ଦିବା ଓ ନିଜେ ଅନ୍ୟର କାମ କରିବାକୁ ମିଳାଇ ପଢ଼ିଲେ ଦୟା ବୁଝାଯାଏ।

ଚରିତ୍ରଚିତ୍ରଣରେ ଏକ ଗୁଣ ପାଇଁ ଏକ ଘଟଣା ଦିଅ।

D. 5. What helped the beggar change his life?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Sergei offered Lushkoff work and later recommended him for copying, a task he could manage. Lushkoff himself gave most credit to Olga. Her grief at his condition and the sight of her doing his hard work moved him more deeply than a lecture. He stopped drinking and eventually became a notary. ସୁଯୋଗ ଓ ମନପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ ଉଭୟ ଦରକାର ହେଲା।

“Changed him” ଅର୍ଥ ଜୀବିକା ଓ ମନୋଭାବ ଉଭୟ ବଦଳିବା। କାମର ସୁଯୋଗ ଜୀବିକାକୁ ସହାୟ ହେଲା।

ଗୋପନରେ ତାଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ କଷ୍ଟ କରିଥିବା ମଣିଷକୁ ଦେଖିବା ନିଜ ଆଚରଣ ବଦଳାଇବାର ପ୍ରେରଣା ଦେଲା।

D. 6. Which character do you prefer, and why?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

I most admire Olga. She did not approve of the beggar’s conduct, but she saw that his weakness was real. Without seeking praise she did a difficult task on his behalf. Her kindness helped him change his own way of life. Lushkoff’s later account reveals how much her actions meant to him. ଅନ୍ୟ ପାତ୍ର ନେଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ କାରଣ ଦିଅ।

ନିଜ ମତକୁ “I admire … because …” ଆକାରରେ ଆରମ୍ଭ କରି ପ୍ରମାଣ ଯୋଡ଼। ଯଦି Sergeiଙ୍କୁ ବାଛ, ପରିଚୟପତ୍ର ଦେବା ତାଙ୍କ ସଂଶୋଧିତ ସହାୟତାର ଏକ ଭଲ ଉଦାହରଣ।

D. 7. Suggest ways of helping people who have to beg.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

People who beg may need immediate food as well as a dependable livelihood. Help is most effective when it takes account of a person’s condition and abilities. Sergei eventually finds work suited to Lushkoff’s skill in writing. Olga shows the importance of personal kindness. Practical opportunities and respectful treatment can work together. ଏହା ମତଭିତ୍ତିକ ଉତ୍ତର।

ତୁରନ୍ତ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ ଓ ଦୀର୍ଘକାଳୀନ କାମ ଅଲଗା ଆବଶ୍ୟକତା ପୂରଣ କରନ୍ତି। ଦୁର୍ବଳ ଲୋକ ପାଇଁ କାଠ ଫାଡ଼ିବା ଅପେକ୍ଷା copying ସମ୍ଭବ ଥିଲା।
