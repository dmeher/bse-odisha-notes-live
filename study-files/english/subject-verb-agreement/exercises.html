Opening comparison · 1–2. Why do the grandmother and the sparrows take different past progressive auxiliaries?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Grandmother is a singular third-person subject, so the finite verb is was in was praying. The sparrows is plural and takes were in were not chirping. The negative word not does not control agreement. ଉଭୟ ବାକ୍ୟ ଅତୀତ କାଳର।

ପକ୍ଷୀଙ୍କ ସଂଖ୍ୟା ଏକରୁ ଅଧିକ ଥିବାରୁ were ବସିଛି। ଜେଜେମା’ଙ୍କ ଦୁଇ କାମ praying ଓ telling ଥିଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ subject ଏକବଚନ।

ଉଭୟ ବାକ୍ୟରେ ଗଠନ past progressive। Praying/ଚାଲିଥିବା କାମର -ing ରୂପ number ବଦଳାଏନାହିଁ।

Subject singularରୁ plural ହେଲେ ବଦଳ ହୁଏ finite beରେ: was → were।

Rule 4(a) · Jury and committee. Why does the same collective noun take a singular verb in one example and a plural verb in the other?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

The jury has selected its chairman and the committee was unanimous treat the members as one group. The jury were divided and the committee were divided focus on members who held different views. ଦଳଟି ଏକକ ହେଲେ ଏକବଚନ କ୍ରିୟା ଓ ଭିନ୍ନ ମତ ଦେଉଥିବା ସଦସ୍ୟମାନେ ମୁଖ୍ୟ ହେଲେ ଏଠାରେ ବହୁବଚନ କ୍ରିୟା ଆସିଛି।

Unanimous ଅର୍ଥ ସମସ୍ତଙ୍କ ଏକ ମତ; divided ଅର୍ଥ ମତ ଅଲଗା। ଏହି ଦୁଇ ଶବ୍ଦରୁ committee ଓ juryକୁ unit ବା ପୃଥକ ସଦସ୍ୟ ଭାବେ ପଢ଼ିବାର ସୂତ୍ର ମିଳେ।
