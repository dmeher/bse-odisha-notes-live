A–B · Listening. How can two speakers understand the same family differently? Read their parts aloud.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

The adult counts living people. The child counts those she still loves. A partner can voice the adult’s surprised questions and another the child’s calm, firm replies. Pause at the repeated number. ଦୁଇ ସ୍ୱରର ପାର୍ଥକ୍ୟ ପଢ଼ିବା ଓ ଶୁଣିବା ବେଳେ ଚିହ୍ନାଅ।

କଥକଙ୍କ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନରେ ଗଣନାର ସନ୍ଦେହ ଶୁଣାଅ। ଝିଅର ପୁନରୁକ୍ତ ଉତ୍ତରକୁ ଶାନ୍ତ ଓ ନିଶ୍ଚିତ ରଖ।

ଏକା ଗାଁର ଦୃଶ୍ୟକୁ ଦୁହେଁ ଭିନ୍ନ ସମ୍ପର୍କରେ ଦେଖନ୍ତି।

G. 1–3. Describe the child of the opening, the girl and her first reply.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. She breathes easily and feels full of life, seemingly untouched by thoughts of death. 2. She is an eight-year-old cottage girl with thick curls, bright eyes and simple country dress. 3. The speaker asks how many children there are in her family; she counts seven. ପ୍ରଥମ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନରେ ସାଧାରଣ ଶିଶୁଚିତ୍ର, ଦ୍ୱିତୀୟରେ ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ ଝିଅଟିର ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା।

ପ୍ରଥମ stanzaର child ଏକ ସାଧାରଣ ଶିଶୁ ବିଷୟରେ ଚିନ୍ତା। ପରେ ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ ଆଠବର୍ଷର ଝିଅ ସହ ଭେଟ ହୁଏ।

ପ୍ରଥମ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ସହ ପରର କଥାବାର୍ତ୍ତା ଯୋଡ଼ା।

G. 4–6. Account for the apparent puzzle in the girl’s count.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

4. Two live at Conway, two are at sea, two are buried nearby, and she lives with her mother. 5. The speaker thinks the dead should be subtracted. 6. The girl still spends time beside their graves and feels close to them.

ଗଣନା ୨+୨+୨+୧ = ୭। କଥକ ସମାଧିରେ ଥିବା ଦୁଇଜଣଙ୍କୁ ବାଦ୍ ଦେଇ ୫ କରନ୍ତି।

ଝିଅ ସେମାନଙ୍କ ସ୍ଥାନ ଭଲ ଜାଣିଥିବାରୁ ସେ ଭୁଲିଯାଇଛି ବୋଲି କହିହେବନାହିଁ।

G. 7–11. Trace Jane’s illness, John’s death and the ending.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

7. Jane lay moaning in pain. 8. Death released her from that pain. 9. John and the girl played around her grave when the grass was dry. 10. John died in snowy weather. 11. The girl insists that there are still seven children.

Jane ଆଗେ, John ପରେ। ମଝିରେ କବର ପାଖରେ ଖେଳିବା ଦୃଶ୍ୟ ଅଛି।

ସବୁଜ ଘାସରୁ ଶୀତର ଦୃଶ୍ୟକୁ ବଦଳ ସମୟ ଗଡ଼ିବାର ଚିହ୍ନ।

H (a). 1–5. Interpret the child’s breathing, vitality, surroundings and surprise.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. Light breathing suggests ease and freedom from anxiety. 2. Each limb seems full of energy. 3. The speaker questions whether such a child can yet grasp death. 4. She is a country girl associated with the cottage and a nearby churchyard. 5. Counting the people she loves seems perfectly natural to her, so his uncertainty surprises her. ଗାଁର ପରିବେଶ ଓ ଆତ୍ମୀୟତା ତା’ର ଭାବକୁ ବୁଝାଏ।

ପିଲାର ଶରୀରର ଶକ୍ତି ଦେଖି କଥକ ଭାବନ୍ତି ଏପରି ପିଲା ମୃତ୍ୟୁକୁ କ’ଣ ଜାଣିପାରେ। ତାଙ୍କ ଏହି ଆରମ୍ଭର ଅନୁମାନକୁ ଝିଅ ନିଜ ପରିବାରର ଘଟଣାରେ ଆହ୍ୱାନ କରେ।

H (a). 6–9. Interpret the green graves, evening light, snow and the adult’s unsuccessful argument.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

6. Grass covers the graves and the place remains part of her familiar world. 7. Even after sunset it is light and fair enough to take her supper outside. 8. Snow locates John’s death in winter. 9. The adult’s repeated words make no difference to her answer. ଘାସ ଓ ବରଫ ଋତୁର ଚିହ୍ନ ମଧ୍ୟ ଦେଉଛି।

ସମାଧି ତା’ ଘର ପାଖରେ; ସେଠି ବସି ସାଧାରଣ କାମ ମଧ୍ୟ କରେ। ଏହି ନିକଟତା ପାଇଁ ପ୍ରାପ୍ତବୟସ୍କଙ୍କ କେବଳ living/dead ଭେଦ ତା’ର ନିଜ ହିସାବକୁ ବୁଝାଇପାରେନାହିଁ।

H (b). Stanzas 1–8. Give the end-rhyme pairs for the first half of the groups’ reading.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1 child/limb are not a full rhyme; breath/death rhyme. 2 girl/curl, said/head. 3 air/fair, clad/glad. 4 Maid/said, be/me. 5 tell/dwell, we/sea. 6 lie/I, brother/mother. 7 dwell/tell, sea/be. 8 reply/lie, we/tree. In stanza 1 it would be misleading to call child/limb rhyming partners. ପ୍ରତି ସ୍ତବକର ଶେଷ ଧ୍ୱନି ଯାଞ୍ଚ କର।

Rhyme ମାନେ ସମାନ ଅର୍ଥ ନୁହେଁ। Breath/death ପରି ଶବ୍ଦର ଶେଷ ଧ୍ୱନି ମିଳେ।

Child/limbର ଶେଷ ଭିନ୍ନ ଶୁଣାଯାଏ; ସବୁ ଯୋଡ଼ାକୁ ଜବରଦସ୍ତ ଏକା ନିୟମରେ ଆଣନାହିଁ।

H (b). Stanzas 9–17. Continue the rhyme lists to the final, five-line stanza.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

9 Maid/laid, alive/five. 10 replied/side; green/seen and more/door are internal rhymes. 11 knit/sit, hem/them. 12 Sir/porringer (weak rhyme), fair/there. 13 Jane/pain, lay/away. 14 laid/played, dry/I. 15 snow/go, slide/side. 16 I/reply, heaven/seven. 17 heaven/seven, still/will; dead (line 1) also echoes said inside the final line. ଦଶମ ସ୍ତବକର ଧାଡ଼ି ମଝିର ମେଳ ଓ ଧାଡ଼ି ଶେଷର ମେଳକୁ ଅଲଗା କର।

I · Group dialogue. Write and perform an exchange between the speaker and the girl.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Speaker: Is the churchyard far from your house? Girl: No, it is only a few steps away. Speaker: What do you do there?

Girl: I take my sewing and sometimes my supper. Speaker: Do you still count Jane and John? Girl: Yes. They are my sister and brother wherever they may be. ଝିଅର ଦୃଢ଼ ଭାବ ଓ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନକାରୀଙ୍କ ଆଶ୍ଚର୍ଯ୍ୟ ଦୁଇଟିକୁ ଅଭିନୟରେ ଶୁଣାଅ।

ସଂଳାପରେ କଥକ ନିଜ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନର ଉତ୍ତର ନିଜେ ଦେବେନାହିଁ। ଝିଅ ନିଜ କାମ—ବସିବା, ସିଲାଇ କରିବା ବା ଗୀତ ଗାଇବା—କହିଲେ ତା’ର ହିସାବ ପାଇଁ ସାକ୍ଷ୍ୟ ମିଳେ।

J. 1–2. Describe the seven and comment on the difference between the adult and child.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. The unnamed girl lives near the churchyard with her mother. Two siblings live at Conway and two are at sea. Jane and John, buried near home, complete the seven. Only those two are named; the mother is outside the count. 2. The adult sees death as a reason to subtract. The child does not deny the deaths: she remembers them clearly, but still shares her daily life with her siblings. I find her count convincing as an expression of love. ନାମ ଦିଆନଥିବା ଚାରିଜଣଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ ନୂଆ ନାମ ଯୋଡ଼ିବା ଆବଶ୍ୟକ ନାହିଁ।

ପ୍ରଥମ ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦରେ ସାତଜଣଙ୍କ ସ୍ଥାନ ସଂକ୍ଷେପରେ ଦିଅ। ଦ୍ୱିତୀୟରେ “The adult counts the living; the girl counts her family” ଭାବକୁ ଏକ କବର ପାଖର ଘଟଣା ଦେଇ ବୁଝାଅ।
