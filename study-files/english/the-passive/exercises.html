Opening comparison · 1a–2b and B(i). What changes in the two active–passive pairs? Mark the present be auxiliaries in the badminton and clothes examples.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

In 1b she met me becomes I was met by her. In 2b Janaki made the attempt becomes the attempt was made by Janaki. The active object becomes the passive subject. In B(i), identify is + played and are + given. The first be-form agrees with singular badminton and the second with plural clothes. ମୁଖ୍ୟ କ୍ରିୟାର past participle ସ୍ଥିର ରହେ।

ସମୟ ଓ ନୂଆ subject ଅନୁସାରେ beର ରୂପ ବଦଳେ।

B(vi) · Perfect progressive question. Why does the book avoid a passive transformation for present and past perfect progressive verbs?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Compare is being played (progressive passive) with has been decorated or had been decorated (perfect passive). To show both meanings mechanically would require has/have/had been being + past participle. The sequence been being is cumbersome and very rarely used. For this exercise keep the perfect progressive in the active voice, or recast the thought while preserving its duration. ଚାଲିଆସୁଥିବା କାମର ଅବଧି ବୁଝାଇବାକୁ ମୂଳ active ବାକ୍ୟ ରଖିଲେ ଅର୍ଥ ସ୍ପଷ୍ଟ ରହେ।

ସରଳ perfect passive ଲେଖିଦେଲେ ଚାଲିଥିବା କାମର ଭାବ ହରାଇପାରେ।

Perfect passiveର been ଓ progressive passiveର beingକୁ ଏକାଠି ରଖିଲେ has/had been being + V3 ଭଳି ଅସ୍ୱାଭାବିକ ଲମ୍ବା group ହୁଏ। ସେହିପାଇଁ ଏହି ପାଠର ଅଭ୍ୟାସରେ ମୂଳ active ରଖାଯାଏ ବା କଥାଟି ଅନ୍ୟ ଭାବରେ କୁହାଯାଏ।

G · Mark the verb forms. Which words carry the passive pattern in the two printed examples?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Was born and was drowned both have was + past participle. Gandhiji was born gives an event in his life. He was drowned presents what happened to him. English regularly uses be born for birth. The second example shows a passive use of drown, which also permits an active use in other contexts. was ଅତୀତ କାଳ ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

born ଓ drowned ଘଟଣାର ଅର୍ଥ ଦିଅନ୍ତି।

Was born ହେଉଛି ଜନ୍ମର ସାଧାରଣ English expression। Was drownedରେ ମଣିଷ ଘଟଣାର ପ୍ରଭାବ ପାଇଲେ।
