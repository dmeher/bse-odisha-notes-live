A · Examples 1–2. In the opening examples, which verb carries tense, and what forms follow it?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1(a) felt is finite, to face is a non-finite infinitive. 1(b) wrote is finite, announcing is a non-finite participle. In 2(a–c), could, may and may respectively are the finite auxiliaries. Their non-finite followers are (a) have, been, laughing, (b) have, been, writing and (c) have, been, written. ଦୀର୍ଘ verb groupରେ ପ୍ରଥମ auxiliaryରେ tense ରହେ, ପରର ସବୁ କ୍ରିୟା ନିଜେ tense ବା subject ଅନୁସାରେ ବଦଳନ୍ତି ନାହିଁ।

Pastକୁ present କଲେ felt → feels ଓ wrote → writes ବଦଳିପାରେ, କିନ୍ତୁ to face ବା announcingର ରୂପ ଅବିକୃତ ରହେ। ଏହି ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ ପରୀକ୍ଷାରୁ finite ଶବ୍ଦଟି ଧରାପଡ଼େ।

A · Examples 3–10. Why can examples 3–6 stand as sentences while the four starred forms cannot? Give one repair for each starred form.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

3 writes, 4 has, 5 is and 6 will are finite. With singular Seema, 7 write cannot on its own express the simple present: use writes. In 8, written requires a finite verb, e.g. has written. In 9 use is writing. For 10, retain the infinitive by adding a finite verb: wants to write. ୭ରେ subject ସହ କ୍ରିୟାର ରୂପ ମେଳିନାହିଁ।

୮–୧୦ରେ participle ବା infinitive ଏକାକୀ ମୁଖ୍ୟ finite verbର କାମ କରିପାରେ ନାହିଁ।

Seema ସହ simple present ଲେଖିଲେ writes; writingକୁ ନିଜେ ରଖିବାକୁ is writing; writtenକୁ perfect କରିବାକୁ has written। Will ପରେ ସେହି ମୂଳ write ଯଥେଷ୍ଟ।

ସୁଧାର କେଉଁଟି ବାଛିବ ତାହା କହିବାକୁ ଚାହୁଁଥିବା ଅର୍ଥ ଧରେ।

A · Examples 11–15. Separate the finite and non-finite forms in the four sentences about Seema, then compare 15(a–b).

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

11 made / write, 12 want / to write, 13 found / written and typed, 14 saw / writing. In each pair the first form is finite. In 15(a–b) wants changes to wanted when the tense changes. The infinitive to write stays the same. ୧୩ରେ written ଓ typed ପତ୍ରଗୁଡ଼ିକର ଅବସ୍ଥା ଜଣାଏ।

୧୪ରେ writing ଦ୍ୱାରା Seemaଙ୍କ ଲେଖିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକୁ ଦେଖିବା କଥା ବୁଝାଏ।

Made someone writeରେ bare form, want someone to writeରେ to-form। Written/typedରେ ଲେଖା ବା ଟାଇପ ହୋଇସାରିଥିବା ଅବସ୍ଥା; saw someone writingରେ ଚାଲିଥିବା କାମ।

ତେଣୁ ଚାରିଟିକୁ ଏକେ infinitive ନାମ ଦିଅନାହିଁ।

Activity 1 (a–e). Identify all the non-finite verb forms in each verb group or complement.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

a been, answering; b have, finished, doing; c have, been, helped; d have, been, studying; e have, been, screened. ଏକ verb groupର ପ୍ରଥମ auxiliary କାଳ ବା modal ଦର୍ଶାଏ। (b)ରେ doing ମଧ୍ୟ finishedର ପରିପୂରକ non-finite form।

ମୂଳ verb groupର ପ୍ରଥମ finite ଶବ୍ଦ ଛାଡ଼ି ପରର ପ୍ରତି ଶବ୍ଦ ଯାଞ୍ଚ କର। “have been helped”ରେ have bare form, been ହେଉଛି beର participle ଓ helped passive participle।

“been studying”ରେ ଶେଷ ଶବ୍ଦ progressive।

Examples 37–40 · Compare. Who is to perform the second action in the four infinitive examples, especially 39 and 40?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

37. I hope to see you: I will do the seeing. 38. He requested me to help him: I am to help. 39. They expected to travel: they are the travellers. 40. They expected me to travel: I am the traveller. ୩୯ ଓ ୪୦ରେ expected ସମାନ।

ତାହା ପରେ me ଯୋଡ଼ା ହେଲେ ଯାତ୍ରା କରିବା ବ୍ୟକ୍ତି ବଦଳିଯାଏ।

I hope to seeରେ I ଦେଖିବି। He requested me to helpରେ ଯାହାକୁ me କୁହାଯାଇଛି ସେ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରିବେ।

Expect ପରେ ମଝିରେ object ଆସିଛି କି ଦେଖିଲେ ଦ୍ୱିତୀୟ କାମର କର୍ତ୍ତା କିଏ ଧରିହେବ।

Activity 2 (a–f). Recover two underlying simple ideas from each sentence.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

a. My friend will get the medal. I want that. b. I did the exercise. She helped me. c. I tell lies. I hate doing that. d. I shall write a story by tomorrow. I expect that. e. You will write a story by tomorrow. I expect that. f. We wrote the story. Our teacher made us do it. (c) ଏକ ବ୍ୟାକରଣ ଅଭ୍ୟାସ।

ଦୁଇ ଅଂଶକୁ ଯୋଡ଼ିଲେ କାହାର କାମ, ଆଉ କିଏ ଚାହୁଁଛି ବା ବାଧ୍ୟ କରୁଛି, ବୁଝାଯାଏ।

ପ୍ରଥମ ଧାରଣାରେ ମୁଖ୍ୟ ଇଚ୍ଛା/ପସନ୍ଦ/ସାହାଯ୍ୟ, ଦ୍ୱିତୀୟରେ କ’ଣ ହେବାକୁ ଚାହୁଁଛନ୍ତି ରଖ। “I want my friend to get the medal” ଭାଙ୍ଗିଲେ ପଦକ ପାଇବ friend ଓ ଚାହେ I।

ଦୁହିଁର subject ଏକ କରନାହିଁ।

Activity 3 (a–f). Name the role of each non-finite clause.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

a to buy… is the object/complement of plans; b to get… completes allow with others; c to save the child is an adjunct of purpose; d her sing a song is the complement of heard (her is its object and does the singing); e To find fault with others is the subject; f to go abroad completes is. ପୃ. ୩୯ ଅନୁସାରେ (b)ରେ କାହାକୁ ଓ କଣ କରିବାକୁ ଅନୁମତି ମିଳୁଛି ଦେଖ।

(c)ରେ Why? ପ୍ରଶ୍ନର ଉତ୍ତର ମିଳେ।

Verbର ଅର୍ଥ ପୂରଣ କରୁଥିବା clause ହଟାଇଲେ କ’ଣ ଯୋଜନା କରାଗଲା ବା କାହାକୁ କ’ଣ ଅନୁମତି ମିଳିଲା ଅସ୍ପଷ୍ଟ ହୁଏ। To save the child କାହିଁକି କାମ ହେଲା ଜଣାଏ, ତେଣୁ ଏଠି purpose।

Activity 4 (a–e). Supply the non-finite clauses.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

(a) me do it; (b) them touch the flowers; (c) the beggar curse the rude boys; (d) anyone to see the files; (e) him to write the letter.

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବୁଝାମଣା — Make, let, hear ପରେ object + bare infinitive। Allow ଓ remind ସହ to ଦରକାର।

Make/let ପରେ object + bare verb। Hear ପରେ କାମଟି ପୂରା ଶୁଣାଗଲେ bare form ହୋଇପାରେ।

Allow/want ପରି ଗଠନରେ object + to-infinitive ରଖ। Them ଓ him ପରେ ସମସ୍ତଠାରେ ଏକେ pattern ନୁହେଁ।

Activity 5 (a–g). Give one possible completion for each gap.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

(a) us make; (b) to meet; (c) us feel; (d) me to phone; (e) us cry; (f) her to study; (g) to come.

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବୁଝାମଣା — କାହାକୁ କିଛି କରିବାକୁ ଦେଉଛନ୍ତି ବା ଉତ୍ସାହିତ କରୁଛନ୍ତି, ସେହି ଅନୁସାରେ object ବାଛ।

Us feel ଓ us cryରେ ଅନୁଭବ କରିବା ବା କାନ୍ଦିବା କର୍ତ୍ତା us ଦ୍ୱାରା ବୁଝାଯାଏ। Me to phone ବା her to studyର to ମୁଖ୍ୟ verbର ଆବଶ୍ୟକତା।

Activity 6 (a–g). Supply the suitable non-finite forms.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

(a) playing; (b) to go; (c) to swim; (d) to study, talking; (e) travelling, to travel; (f) read; (g) to see.

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବୁଝାମଣା — ମୁଖ୍ୟ କ୍ରିୟା ପର ରୂପ ନିର୍ଣ୍ଣୟ କରେ। Enjoy ଓ mind ପରେ -ing, hope ପରେ to-infinitive।

Travelling/to travel ଥିବା ଦୁଇ ସ୍ଥାନରେ ପୂର୍ବ verb ଏକେ ନୁହେଁ।

Activity 7 (a–h). Select and supply an infinitive with or without to.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

a laugh; b to go; c walk; d sleep; e clean; f to hear; g to come; h climb. Make/let/see ପରେ bare infinitive। Want ଓ persuade ପରେ ବ୍ୟକ୍ତିକୁ ସୂଚାଉଥିବା object ଏବଂ to-infinitive।
