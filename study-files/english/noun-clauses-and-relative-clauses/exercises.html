Opening comparison · 1a–c, 2a–b. Compare the two cinema statements and the two actions involving the letter. Which clauses can stand alone? Does exchanging the actions preserve sense?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1a and 1b link two self-contained statements with but: the family went to the cinema and Mohan stayed home. In 1c, Mohan stayed at home is the main clause. The when-clause tells when and depends on it. In 2a, the same subject seals and then posts the letter. In 2b, reversing the verbs puts posting first, which makes no practical sense for this letter. ସଂଯୋଜକ ବଦଳିଲେ clauseର ସମ୍ପର୍କ ବଦଳେ।

ଗଠନ ଠିକ୍ ଥିଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ ଘଟଣାକ୍ରମ ଭୁଲ୍ ହୋଇପାରେ।

But ଦୁଇଟି ପୂରା କଥା ଯୋଡ଼େ, ତେଣୁ ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକର ନିଜ subject ଓ finite verb ମିଳେ। Letterକୁ seal ଓ post କରିବା କ୍ରିୟାରେ ସମୟକ୍ରମ ଅଛି; ବ୍ୟାକରଣରେ ଦୁଇଟି statement possible ହୋଇଥିଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ ଅଦଳବଦଳରେ ଅର୍ଥ ବଦଳେ।

Opening comparison · 3a–c. In the three after-constructions, what is the main clause and what is the other clause? How do 2a and 3a differ?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

In 3a–c the main action is he posted the letter. The after-clause makes the sealing earlier, regardless of word order. In 3a and 3b, sealing is non-finite and its understood subject is he. In 3c, had in he had sealed is finite and the subject he is explicit. The book treats the actions in 2a as coordinated. In 3a, the sealing is subordinate to the posting. after ଥିବା ଅଂଶଟି ସମୟ ଜଣାଏ, ସ୍ୱତନ୍ତ୍ର ମୁଖ୍ୟ ବାକ୍ୟ ହୋଇ ରହେ ନାହିଁ।

After ଯେଉଁ କାମ ସହ ଯୋଡ଼ା ସେହି କାମ ପ୍ରଥମେ। Clauseଟି ପରେ ଲେଖା ହୋଇଥିଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ sealing ଆଗରୁ ହୁଏ।

Coordinating and କେବଳ ଦୁଇ କାମ ଯୋଡ଼େ, after ସମୟର ସମ୍ପର୍କକୁ ସ୍ପଷ୍ଟ କରେ।

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

S: Where have you been?; Humpty Dumpty sat on a wall; The clock struck one; Who pulled her out?; Jack fell down. P: Under the queen’s chair; A stitch in time; All the king’s horses; Like a diamond in the sky; up the hill; Pussy in the well; a nice fairy story. C: After sealing the letter; to fetch a pail of water; And Jill came tumbling after (a coordinate clause in the rhyme). ଶେଷ ଉଦାହରଣଟି and ସହ ପୂର୍ବ କଥାକୁ ଯୋଡ଼େ।

ଦୁଇଟି non-finite clause ଏକା ପୂରା ବାକ୍ୟ ହୋଇପାରନ୍ତି ନାହିଁ।

ସ୍ୱାଧୀନ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ “Who pulled her out?” ଏକ sentence, କାରଣ ତା’ର ଅର୍ଥ ଓ finite verb ଦୁହେଁ ଅଛି। “Under the …” ପରି phraseରେ finite verb ନାହିଁ।

Dependent clauseରେ verb ଥାଏ, ତଥାପି ମୁଖ୍ୟ କଥା ଲାଗେ।

Opening Activity 2 · first nine. Use the slash to distinguish the main-clause frame from its subordinate clause.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

I cannot express / how sorry I am. Do you know / when the next train arrives? Jack fell down / no subordinate clause. All … is not gold / that glitters. All depends on … / how the work is done. He says / that his mother is ill. It is feared / that he will not help us. He told me / that he was ready. It is clear / that he is honest. ଚତୁର୍ଥରେ relative clause ମୁଖ୍ୟ ବାକ୍ୟର ମଝିରେ ଆସେ।

ତୃତୀୟର and ପରର ଅଂଶ coordinate, ଏହି ପ୍ରଶ୍ନରେ ତାହାକୁ ଚିହ୍ନଟ କରିବା ମାଗି ନାହିଁ।

“Do you know when the next train arrives?”ରେ ମୂଳ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ Do you know …? ଭିତର ପ୍ରଶ୍ନରେ subject train ଆଗେ ଓ arrives ପରେ।

Jack fell downରେ ଦ୍ୱିତୀୟ clause ଖୋଜିବାର କାରଣ ନାହିଁ।

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Relative clauses · Activity 3. Define the twelve terms with a relative clause.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

A driver is a person who drives a vehicle; a conductor, one who collects fares; an actor, one who plays a part; a teacher, one who helps learners; a journalist, one who reports news; a plumber, one who repairs pipes; a farmer, one who grows crops; a doctor, one who treats illness; a patient, one who receives treatment.
