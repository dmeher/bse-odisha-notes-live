D. 1. How did Grandfather acquire Timothy?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Timothy was a little tiger cub, about eighteen inches long. Grandfather had joined a hunting party because he knew the forest well. While the hunters found no tiger, he noticed the cub hiding among the roots of a banyan tree. He picked it up and brought it home after the camp broke up. ଅନ୍ୟ ଶିକାରୀଙ୍କ ଫଳ ଓ ଜେଜେଙ୍କ ଆବିଷ୍କାରର ଫରକ ଦେଖ।

“Acquired” କହିଲେ ଛୁଆଟି କିପରି ତାଙ୍କ ପାଖକୁ ଆସିଲା ତାହା ଲାଗିବ। ଏଥିରେ ଟଙ୍କା ଦେଇ କିଣିବା ବା ଶିକାର କରିବା ନାହିଁ।

ସ୍ଥାନ, ଛୁଆର ଅବସ୍ଥା ଓ ତାଙ୍କ କାମକୁ କ୍ରମରେ ରଖ।

D. 2. Describe the cub’s companions and care at home.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Mahmoud, the cook, first fed Timothy milk from a bottle. When it proved too rich, the family gave him raw mutton and cod liver oil and later pigeons and rabbits. Toto sometimes teased him. A small puppy frightened him at first but eventually rested on his back. He also played with the narrator. ସମୟ ଅନୁସାରେ ଖାଦ୍ୟ ଓ ସାଙ୍ଗମାନଙ୍କ କଥା ଲେଖ।

Companions କହିଲେ ଖେଳସାଥୀ, care କହିଲେ ଖାଦ୍ୟ ଓ ରହିବାର ଯତ୍ନ। Toto ଓ କୁକୁରଛୁଆକୁ ପ୍ରଥମ ଭାଗରେ, Mahmoudଙ୍କ ଖୁଆଇବାକୁ ଦ୍ୱିତୀୟରେ ରଖିଲେ ଉଭୟ ଅଂଶର ଉତ୍ତର ମିଳେ।

D. 3. Why did Grandfather transfer Timothy to a zoo?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

At about six months Timothy became harder to manage. Outdoors he tried to stalk people’s pets. Noises from the poultry house and feathers on the veranda caused further concern. When he began following Mahmoud about the house as though hunting him, Grandfather decided that keeping him at home was no longer safe. ନିଷ୍ପତ୍ତିର ଶେଷ କାରଣ Mahmoudଙ୍କ ସୁରକ୍ଷା।

‘Stalk’ ପଛରେ ଲୁଚିଲୁଚି ଅନୁସରଣ କରିବା ବୁଝାଏ। ବାଘର ଏହି ବଦଳକୁ ଘରର ପୁରୁଣା ହାସଖୁସି ସହ ମିଳାଇଲେ ନିଷ୍ପତ୍ତିର କାରଣ ସ୍ପଷ୍ଟ।

ବିଚ୍ଛେଦର କାରଣ ସ୍ନେହ କମିଯିବା ନଥିଲା।

D. 4. What did Grandfather do beside the cage?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Believing that he had recognised Timothy, Grandfather reached through the bars and stroked the tiger’s forehead. He tickled its ear and used the familiar gesture that had once quietened his pet. When a leopard in the next cage frightened the tiger, he tried to drive it away and sought the superintendent’s help. ସେ ବାଘଟିକୁ Timothy ବୋଲି ଭାବୁଥିଲେ।

କାମଗୁଡ଼ିକ ସେ କ’ଣ ବିଶ୍ୱାସ କରୁଥିଲେ ଦେଖାଏ। ପୂର୍ବରୁ ଚିହ୍ନା ପଶୁ ଭାବିଲେ ମାତ୍ର ସେ ଏପରି ନିର୍ଭୟ ଆଦର କରିବାକୁ ଆଗେଇବେ।

ପରିଚୟଟି ଏଠି ତାଙ୍କ ଅନୁମାନ ବୋଲି ରଖ।

D. 5. How did the tiger respond to Grandfather?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

The tiger came close enough for Grandfather to hold its head and stroke it. It licked his hands as an affectionate pet might do. When the neighbouring leopard snarled or rushed at the bars, however, the tiger slipped back into its corner. Those responses helped sustain Grandfather’s mistaken belief that this was Timothy. ବାଘର ପ୍ରତିକ୍ରିୟା ଜେଜେଙ୍କୁ ଭୁଲ ଧାରଣା ଦେଲା।

ଏହି ପ୍ରଶ୍ନର କର୍ତ୍ତା tiger, ଆଗର ପ୍ରଶ୍ନରେ Grandfather। ମୁଣ୍ଡ ପାଖକୁ ଆଣିବା ଓ ହାତ ଚାଟିବା ପରି ପ୍ରତିକ୍ରିୟା ଲେଖ।

ମଣିଷ ତାକୁ ଆଦର କଲେ ବୋଲି ମାତ୍ର ଲେଖିଲେ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ବଦଳିଯାଏ।

D. 6. How did the visit end for Grandfather?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

A keeper who remembered Timothy told Grandfather that his pet had died of pneumonia two months before. The animal in the cage was a recently captured, dangerous tiger. Grandfather drew back his hand. Still moved by the encounter, he addressed the animal as Timothy once more, wished it good night and walked away. ରକ୍ଷକଙ୍କ ଖବର ଓ ଜେଜେଙ୍କ ଶେଷ କଥାକୁ ଯୋଡ଼।

ପୁରୁଣା keeper ସମୟ ଓ ରୋଗର ଖବର ଜାଣନ୍ତି। ତେଣୁ ପୁରୁଣା ସ୍ମୃତି ଉପରେ ଆଧାରିତ ଚିହ୍ନଟକୁ ତାଙ୍କ ଖବର ସୁଧାରିଦିଏ।

ଶେଷରେ ମଧ୍ୟ ବିଦାୟ ଜଣାଇବା Grandfatherଙ୍କ ସ୍ନେହକୁ ଦେଖାଏ।
