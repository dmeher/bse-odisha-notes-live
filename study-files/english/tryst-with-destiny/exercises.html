A · Lead-in. When and before whom was the speech delivered?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Nehru addressed the Constituent Assembly at midnight between 14 and 15 August 1947 as India became independent. The address links celebration with the work of building a democratic country. ଭାଷଣରେ midnight ଓ pledgeର ଗୁରୁତ୍ୱ ଏହି ପୃଷ୍ଠଭୂମିରୁ ବୁଝାଯାଏ।

ଜାଗା ଓ ଶ୍ରୋତା Constituent Assembly, କିନ୍ତୁ “we”ର ଆହ୍ୱାନ ସମଗ୍ର ଦେଶ ପାଇଁ। ୧୪ରୁ ୧୫ ଅଗଷ୍ଟ ମଧ୍ୟରାତ୍ରି ଲେଖିଲେ ଦୁଇ ତାରିଖର ସମ୍ପର୍କ ସ୍ପଷ୍ଟ ହୁଏ।

E. 1–6. Explain the pledge, awakening, historic moment, quest, release and challenge.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. The pledge is to serve India, her people and humanity. 2. India takes charge of its life as a free country. 3. The passing of an old age and the birth of an independent nation is rare. 4. Across good and bad times India seeks to realise the ideals that give her strength. 5. Independence ends foreign rule and allows India to rediscover herself. 6. The challenge is to turn this opportunity into lasting freedom and opportunity for ordinary people. ସ୍ୱାଧୀନତା ଲାଭକୁ ଭବିଷ୍ୟତ କାମର ଆରମ୍ଭ ବୋଲି ଲେଖକ ଦେଖିଛନ୍ତି।

Pledge ମାତ୍ର ଆଗ ସମୟର ପ୍ରତିଜ୍ଞା। Independence ସେଥିରୁ ଗୋଟିଏ ଅଂଶ ପୂରଣ କରେ, କିନ୍ତୁ ଲୋକଙ୍କ ସେବା ଚାଲିବାକୁ ଅଛି।

ତେଣୁ awakening ସହ challenge ଏକାଠି ରହିଛି।

E. 7–9. Explain responsibility, the new star and the spirit of freedom.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

7. Responsibility rests on the Assembly as representative of the people. 8. The new star represents freedom and hope. 9. The people must act with discipline and accept the burdens of independence.

Assembly ଲୋକଙ୍କ ପ୍ରତିନିଧି ହୋଇଥିବାରୁ ଦାୟିତ୍ୱ ତାହା ପାଖରେ ଆସେ। ‘New star’ ଭୌତିକ ତାରା ନୁହେଁ; ଅନ୍ୟଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ ମଧ୍ୟ ଆଶା ଦେଉଥିବା ନୂଆ ସ୍ୱାଧୀନତା।

E. 10–11. What does Nehru remember about Gandhi and the people across the border?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

10. Gandhi’s courage, faith, strength and humility inspired the struggle. 11. Political boundaries have separated people who are still regarded as brothers and sisters. Nehru promises to share their sorrow or happiness. ରାଜନୈତିକ ବିଭାଜନ ପରେ ମଧ୍ୟ ମାନବୀୟ ସମ୍ପର୍କ ରହିଛି।

Gandhiଙ୍କ ଗୁଣ ମନେ ପକାଇବା ଆନ୍ଦୋଳନର ନୈତିକ ଦିଗକୁ ସମ୍ମାନ। ସୀମାପାରର ଲୋକଙ୍କୁ ମନେ ପକାଇବା ରାଜନୈତିକ ବିଭାଜନ ସତ୍ତ୍ୱେ ପୁରୁଣା ସମ୍ପର୍କର ସ୍ୱୀକୃତି।

E. 12–15. What work remains for India and the world?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

12. Build a prosperous, democratic nation with justice and opportunity. 13. Relieve poverty, ignorance and disease through continued work. 14. Cooperate for peace, freedom and democracy. 15. Dedicate ourselves to service of the motherland.

ଭାରତ ମଧ୍ୟରେ ଦାରିଦ୍ର୍ୟ ଓ ଅଜ୍ଞତା କମାଇବା ଏକ କ୍ଷେତ୍ର। ବିଶ୍ୱ ସହ ଶାନ୍ତି ଓ ସହଯୋଗ ଅନ୍ୟ କ୍ଷେତ୍ର।

ଦୁଇଟିରେ ଲୋକଙ୍କ ଭଲ ଏକ ମୂଳ ଉଦ୍ଦେଶ୍ୟ।

F. 1–5. Use the speech to discuss the work remaining after independence.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. End poverty, ignorance, disease and unequal opportunity and build democratic institutions. 2. At the moment of the speech suffering still exists; Gandhi’s aim remains unfinished. 3. Freedom has only begun and earlier disadvantages and divisions remain. 4. Sorrow, poverty, ignorance and communal narrowness are among the clouds. 5. A progressive nation gives all its citizens rights, opportunities and justice and works for peace. ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନର ଅବସ୍ଥା ବିଷୟରେ ଶ୍ରେଣୀର ମତ ଦେଲେ ତାହାକୁ ୧୯୪୭ର ଭାଷଣରୁ ଅଲଗା କର।

“Well begun” ଭାବକୁ completed ଭାବେ ଲେଖନାହିଁ। ଭାଷଣ ସମୟରେ ରାଜନୈତିକ ସ୍ୱାଧୀନତା ମିଳୁଛି; ନ୍ୟାୟ ଓ ସୁଯୋଗକୁ ଦୈନନ୍ଦିନ ଜୀବନରେ ଆଣିବା ପରର ସମୂହ କାମ।

G. 1. Match the five words to the meanings in the other column.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

redeem → fulfil; pledge → promise; sovereign → supreme; fateful → crucial; embody → symbolize. Redeem a pledge ହେଉଛି ଦିଆଯାଇଥିବା ପ୍ରତିଜ୍ଞା ପୂରଣ କରିବା।

Redeem ସହ pledge ବସିଲେ promise ପୂରଣ କରିବାର ଅର୍ଥ। Sovereign ସହ ଦେଶର ନିଜ ନିଷ୍ପତ୍ତିର କ୍ଷମତା ଯୋଡ଼।

Fateful ହେଉଛି ଦୂରପ୍ରସାରୀ ଫଳ ଥିବା, କେବଳ ଦୁଃଖଦ ନୁହେଁ।

G. 2–4. Form verbs, replace the expressions in bold, then use each phrase.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

2. utter: She uttered a warning. dedicate: We dedicate our time to study. achieve: They achieved their aim. embody: Her deeds embody kindness. cloud: Smoke clouds the sky. 3. (a) Nehru’s word for very important is fateful: Kamala’s decision was fateful. (b) My parents rejoiced in my examination success. (c) The volunteers helped the sorrow-stricken flood victims. (d) We should not stray from Gandhiji’s message. (e) The boy’s humility struck me. 4. (a) We take a pledge to help. (b) Duties rest upon us. (c) We will work as long as help is needed. (d) The wind may blow out the lamp. (e) Floods can cut off a village. କାମ ବୁଝାଇବା ପାଇଁ nounରୁ verb ଚୟନ କର; ନୂଆ ବାକ୍ୟରେ ତାହାର କାଳ ମେଳାଅ।

Word class ପାଇଁ “they can …” ପରେ verb ବସାଅ। Achieve an aim ଓ embody an idealରେ object ଭିନ୍ନ।

a. The headmaster gave a certificate to the girl who had saved a child’s life. b. My father bought an apartment where we shall stay. c. The boy met me in the library where I was waiting for him. d. I · Discussion. Present one problem raised by Nehru and exchange a question with another group.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Speaker: Unequal opportunity keeps some people from taking part in common life. In school, our group could share reading time and explain a difficult lesson to someone who needs help. Listener: How would you know who wants to join?

Speaker: We could invite everyone privately and let people choose. ଦଳ ନିଜ ପ୍ରସ୍ତାବ ଦେଉ ଓ ଅନ୍ୟ ଦଳର ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ଶୁଣୁ।

ଅନ୍ୟ ଦଳ “Who would benefit?” ପଚାରିଲେ “Pupils who do not get enough reading time” ଭଳି ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ ଉତ୍ତର ଦିଅ। Opportunityକୁ ଗୋଟିଏ କରିପାରିବା କାମରେ ବ୍ୟାଖ୍ୟା କରିହୁଏ।

J · Write-up. Write a brief problem–cause–action account on your chosen issue.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Equal opportunity begins with access to learning. In his address Nehru speaks of the need to end ignorance. A pupil who cannot get help with a difficult lesson may fall behind even when willing to work. A reading circle could let classmates work together and share explanations. We could start with one lesson, invite everyone and ask what help made a difference. ଏଠାରେ ସ୍ଥାନୀୟ ପ୍ରସ୍ତାବ ନମୁନା; ନିଜ ଦଳର ଆଲୋଚନାରୁ ବିବରଣୀ ଭର।

ପ୍ରଥମ ବାକ୍ୟ ମୁଖ୍ୟ ସମସ୍ୟା, ମଝିରେ ତାହା ଲୋକଙ୍କୁ କିପରି ପ୍ରଭାବିତ କରେ, ଶେଷରେ କ’ଣ କରିହେବ ରଖ। ନିଜ ପ୍ରସ୍ତାବକୁ Nehruଙ୍କ ସିଧା ଉକ୍ତି ବୋଲି ଲେଖନାହିଁ।
