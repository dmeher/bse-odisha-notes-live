A–B · Listening. Identify two sounds and a movement in the speaker’s opening description.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Bicker suggests a noisy downhill rush; chatter later makes stones and water sound like speech. Sally suggests a sudden start. Partner A can read the opening briskly, then slow down for linger and loiter later. ଆରମ୍ଭର ଗତି ଓ ଶେଷର ଧୀର ସ୍ୱରକୁ ମୁଖରେ ପଢ଼ି ତୁଳନା କର।

Bicker ଓ chatter ପଢ଼ିଲେ ଝରଣାର ଶବ୍ଦ ଶୁଣିହୁଏ। Sally ତାହାର ହଠାତ୍ ଆଗକୁ ବାହାରିବାକୁ ଦେଖାଏ।

ଶବ୍ଦ ଓ ଗତି ପାଇଁ ପୃଥକ ଉଦାହରଣ ଦିଅ।

F. 1–4. Trace the brook from its source to its meeting with the river.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. It comes from the haunts of coot and hern. 2. It flashes into the light among the ferns. 3. It passes hills, ridges, villages, a town and bridges. 4. It joins the river by Philip’s farm.

Source ପାଇଁ ପକ୍ଷୀ ରହୁଥିବା ସ୍ଥାନ, route ପାଇଁ hills ଓ villages, destination ପାଇଁ river ରଖ। Brook riverରେ ମିଶିଲା ପରେ ଏହି ପଥବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା ସରେ।

F. 5–7. Explain the bank, chatter and quiet movement.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

5. Its winding current gradually wears away the banks. 6. Repetition imitates water making quick sounds over stones. 7. “Steal” and “slide” suggest its quiet, smooth movement.

ପାଣିରେ କୂଳ କ୍ଷୟ ଦୀର୍ଘ ସମୟର ଫଳ। Chatter ତତ୍କ୍ଷଣାତ୍ ଶୁଣାଯିବା ଶବ୍ଦ।

Steal by ଏଠି ଚୋରିର ଅର୍ଥ ଦିଏନାହିଁ; ଅଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟରେ ମୃଦୁ ଭାବରେ ଚାଲିବା ବୁଝାଏ।

F. 8–9. Explain the dancing sunbeam and reorder each printed set of lines.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

8. Sunlight falls in a net-like pattern across the shallow water; ripples make its reflection appear to dance. 9(i) 4 → 1 → 3 → 2: murmuring under the stars, brambles, shingly bars, cresses. 9(ii) 2 → 1 → 4 → 3: the brook curves, joins the river, mentions passing people and then its own continuing flow. ସଂଖ୍ୟା ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ ଖଣ୍ଡର ଛପା ଧାଡ଼ିର କ୍ରମକୁ ସୂଚାଏ।

ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟର ସ୍ଥାନ ଦ୍ରୁତ ନାଚୁନାହିଁ। ଚଳନ୍ତା ଜଳର ପ୍ରତିଫଳନରୁ dance ଚିତ୍ର ହୁଏ।

ସଜାଣି ଅଂଶରେ ଗୋଟିଏ ପଙ୍କ୍ତିର subject ପର ପଙ୍କ୍ତିର verb ସହ ମିଳୁଛି କି ପରଖ।

G (a). 1–6. Explain motion, sound, the river and the recurring refrain.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. Here bicker describes the noisy descent; it makes us hear the water. 2. The stream winds in bends instead of taking a straight course. 3. Its ripples over stones and pebbles sound like quick speech. 4. Brimming suggests a river full of water. 5. I picture a narrow, lively brook entering a broad, full channel. 6. The refrain brings us back to the difference between a human lifetime and the brook’s continuing course. ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ୫ର ଚିତ୍ର ନିଜର ହୋଇପାରେ; ଶବ୍ଦରୁ ପ୍ରମାଣ ଦିଅ।

ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ motion word ସହ ପଥର ପ୍ରକୃତି ଯୋଡ଼: ଢାଲରେ bicker, ବକ୍ରପଥରେ wind। ପୁନରୁକ୍ତ refrain ଉତ୍ତରରେ ଏକ ପିଢ଼ିର ମଣିଷ ଆସି ଚାଲିଗଲେ ବି ଜଳପ୍ରବାହ ଚାଲିଥିବା ଲେଖ।

G (b). 1–4. Choose the answers about the journey, fish, speaker and duration.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1 d, a human life; 2 i, the brook is full of life; 3 iv, the brook speaks; 4 iii, eternal, in the poem’s comparison. ଜୀବନ ସହ ଯାତ୍ରା ତୁଳନୀୟ ହେଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ ମନୁଷ୍ୟ ଓ ପ୍ରବାହର ସ୍ଥାୟିତ୍ୱ ଅଲଗା।

Fish ଉଲ୍ଲେଖ ଜଳ ଭିତର ଜୀବନର ପ୍ରମାଣ। “I” ଆମକୁ ବୁଝାଏ ଝରଣା ନିଜେ କହୁଛି।

Eternalକୁ ଏଠି କବିତାର ମାନବଜୀବନ ସହ ତୁଳନା ଭାବେ ପଢ଼।

G (c) · Rhymes and personification. Name seven rhyming pairs and write a six-line poem spoken by an object.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

hern/fern, sally/valley, down/town, ridges/bridges, flow/go, ways/bays, trebles/pebbles. A lamp speaks: I wait beside your chair at night. / You open books beneath my light. / I see the letters line by line. / Your pencil moves beside my shine. / At dawn you close the book with care. / I watch the sunrise from my chair. ଜଡ଼ବସ୍ତୁ ନିଜେ କହୁଛି ବୋଲି I ବ୍ୟବହାର କରାଯାଇଛି।

ଏକ ବସ୍ତୁ ପ୍ରଥମ ପୁରୁଷରେ କଥା କହିଲେ personification ହୁଏ। ନିଜ ଛଅ ପଙ୍କ୍ତିରେ ସେ ବସ୍ତୁ କ’ଣ କରେ ଓ କ’ଣ ଦେଖେ ତାହା ଲେଖି ଶେଷ ଶବ୍ଦଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ଯୋଡ଼ାରେ ଶୁଣ।

H (i). Match children’s questions to stanzas a reader can use as replies.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Where do you rise? — stanza 1. What places do you pass? — stanza 2. What sounds do you make over stones? — stanza 4. Which fish swim with you? — stanza 7. What do you do under the moon? — stanza 12. Where are you going at the end? — stanza 13. ଉତ୍ତରଦାତା ନିଜ ପୁସ୍ତକର ସମ୍ପୃକ୍ତ ସ୍ତବକଟି ପଢ଼ିବେ।

Where ପାଇଁ ସ୍ଥାନ ଥିବା stanza, what sound ପାଇଁ chatter ଆଦି ଥିବା stanza ବାଛ। ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ଓ ଉତ୍ତରର ପ୍ରଥମ ଶବ୍ଦ ଏକା ନଥିଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ ଏହି ସମ୍ପର୍କ ମିଳେ।

H (ii). Complete and enact the conversation between a child and a brook.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Child: Who are you? Brook: I am a small stream. Child: Where are you going?

Brook: To join the river. Child: Where will you meet it? Brook: Near Philip’s farm. Child: What do you see en route?

Brook: I pass hills, bridges, fields and flowers. Child: How long will you flow? Brook: In my song I flow for ever. ନିଜ ଭାଷାରେ କଥୋପକଥନ କରି ପ୍ରଥମ ପୁରୁଷର ସ୍ୱର ରଖ।

Child ପ୍ରଶ୍ନରେ you କହିବ, brook ଉତ୍ତରରେ I କହିବ। “I pass …” ପରେ ପଥର ସ୍ଥାନ ଓ “I join …” ପରେ river ରଖିଲେ ସଂଳାପ ଯାତ୍ରାର କ୍ରମ ଦେଖାଏ।

I (a), (b). Write an object’s self-description and a rhyming stanza.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

a. I am a blackboard. All day I carry questions and diagrams. At the end of the lesson someone wipes me clean, and the next class gives me a new beginning. b. I am a pen that leaves a trail, / I ride each line as boats might sail. / You rest my point when day is done, / I wake to greet the morning sun. ଶେଷ ଧ୍ୱନି trail/sail ଓ done/sun ମିଳିଛି।

Blackboard ନିଜ ପୃଷ୍ଠରେ ଲେଖା ଓ ପୋଛାଯିବା କହିପାରେ। ଏଭଳି ବସ୍ତୁ ସହ ମେଳ ଥିବା ଦୁଇ କାମ ବାଛ, ପରେ ଏକ ଯୋଡ଼ା rhyming end words ଖୋଜ।
