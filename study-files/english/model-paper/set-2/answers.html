Write an essay in about 200 words on “Generosity Without Recognition”.

Generosity Without Recognition A kind act usually seems easy to recognise when we know who performed it. Lencho’s story shows how different the same act can look to a person who has only part of the evidence. A hailstorm destroys the farmer’s crop, and his family faces hunger until another crop can be grown. His letter asking for help reaches the people at the post office. At first the address seems amusing. The postmaster then notices the faith behind it. He knows that an answer on paper will not solve the practical problem. His own contribution, together with money collected from others, makes it possible to send the farmer seventy pesos. The gift is smaller than the sum requested, but it represents real effort by several people. Lencho sees the sum and draws a surprising conclusion. He trusts the sender whose name appears on the reply, yet suspects the men who handled it. We understand the mistake because we have followed both sides of the story. His accusation does not cancel their generosity. Their purpose was to help a family in need, and their action still has value even though the person they helped fails to thank them.

Write an essay in about 200 words on “The Value of Looking Closely”.

The Value of Looking Closely A familiar place can become interesting when we give it attention. A little flower opening or a bird lifting its wings may occupy very little space, yet each has a colour, shape or movement worth noticing. The poem about bright and beautiful things asks us to look in just this way. Its speaker does not stop with small objects. A mountain changes colour in the evening light. A river runs below it, and the sky looks different as morning comes. The air also changes with the seasons. The warmth of the summer sun means something different when we remember the cold wind of winter. Such contrasts make the description more vivid than a simple list of beautiful objects. Looking closely also changes our understanding of ourselves. The meadow is a place where people play, and the plants beside the water can be gathered. We are present in the landscape that we admire. At the end, the speaker thinks of the eyes that make observation possible and the lips that can express thanks. The movement from looking to speaking is important. Appreciation begins when a person notices something particular and grows when that discovery can be put into words.

Write an essay in about 200 words on “The Duty of a Judge”.

The Duty of a Judge A judge must listen to a dispute even when a personal relationship makes listening difficult. In the village story, the old aunt chooses Algu although he is Jumman’s friend. She trusts that the duty of the position will matter more to him than private affection. Algu has good reason to hesitate. An honest ruling may cost him an old friendship.
