J (ii). Summary. Summarise the passage on the slow over-rate in about 65–70 words.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

India took almost twenty-eight extra minutes to complete the innings. Vinay Kumar’s cramp, the time spent attending to him and Australia’s late hitting contributed to the delay. Under the ICC policy accepted in 2011, two minor over-rate offences in the same format within twelve months could mean a one-match suspension for the captain. Dhoni acknowledged receiving regular updates during the match and accepted the charge and penalties. ମୁଖ୍ୟ ତଥ୍ୟ ଚାରିଟି: ବିଳମ୍ବ, ତାହାର କାରଣ, ନିୟମ ଓ ଅଧିନାୟକଙ୍କ ଉତ୍ତର।

ସାରାଂଶରେ extra minutesକୁ ମୁଖ୍ୟ ତଥ୍ୟ କର। Cramp ଓ ଖେଳାଳିଙ୍କୁ ସହାୟତା ଯେପରି ଖେଳ ବିଳମ୍ବିତ କଲା ତାହାକୁ ଏକାଠି କହ।
