୧. How does the speaker connect personal habits and citizenship?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Good health helps a person work, while truthfulness, kindness and fairness guide the use of that ability. Wise habits and loyalty to public duties make a school-leaver a dependable citizen. ନିଜ ଅଭ୍ୟାସ ଭଲ ହେଲେ ସାମାଜିକ କର୍ତ୍ତବ୍ୟ ପାଳନ ସହଜ ହୁଏ।

୨. Why should a pupil seek advice and continue learning after leaving school?

ଆଦର୍ଶ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନପତ୍ର · ସେଟ୍ ୧, ଭାଗ ୨, ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ୨ (i) (୨.୫ ନମ୍ବର)

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

A pupil may be unsure which occupation offers a worthwhile future. A teacher can help the pupil make a careful choice. Once work begins, effort and continued learning are still needed for success. ପାଠଶାଳା ଛାଡ଼ିଲେ ଅଧ୍ୟୟନର ଶେଷ ହୁଏ ନାହିଁ।

୩. How does the farewell address define a successful school-leaver?

ଆଦର୍ଶ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନପତ୍ର · ସେଟ୍ ୨, ଭାଗ ୨, ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ୧ (a) (୫ ନମ୍ବର)

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

The speaker expects school-leavers to keep learning and to choose work with care. Success requires health, so regular habits and useful recreation matter. It also requires an honest character: a pupil should be fair to rivals and helpful to the weak. Books and good company can teach wise use of leisure. These habits help one become a credit to the family and a responsible citizen. ସଫଳତାରେ ପେଶା, ସ୍ୱାସ୍ଥ୍ୟ ଓ ଚରିତ୍ର ତିନୋଟି ଦିଗ ରହେ।

୪. Explain why good company matters in leisure hours.

ଆଦର୍ଶ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନପତ୍ର · ସେଟ୍ ୨, ଭାଗ ୨, ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ୨ (i) · ବିକଳ୍ପ ୧ (୨.୫ ନମ୍ବର)

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Companions can influence a person’s habits and values. The speaker recommends associations that encourage good conduct, alongside reading and worthwhile hobbies.

୫. Why does the farewell address give advice about both work and recreation?

ଆଦର୍ଶ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନପତ୍ର · ସେଟ୍ ୩, ଭାଗ ୨, ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ୧ (a) · ବିକଳ୍ପ ୧ (୫ ନମ୍ବର)

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Careful choice of work gives a pupil a direction, and further study is needed to succeed in that work. Recreation contributes to health and can prevent idleness. A useful hobby, a good book or a good companion can strengthen habits that matter at work and in public life. The advice treats the person’s working and leisure hours as parts of the same life.

୬. What makes character a possession that must be protected?

ଆଦର୍ଶ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନପତ୍ର · ସେଟ୍ ୪, ଭାଗ ୨, ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ୨ (i) (୨.୫ ନମ୍ବର)

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Character directs the way a person uses strength and opportunity. Truthfulness, fairness and care for others can earn trust, while gambling or selfish conduct can damage it. ଚରିତ୍ର ଆଚରଣ ମାଧ୍ୟମରେ ପ୍ରକାଶ ପାଏ।

୭. How are a good profession, a sound body and a good character connected in “School’s Goodbye”?

ଆଦର୍ଶ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନପତ୍ର · ସେଟ୍ ୫, ଭାଗ ୨, ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ୧ (a) (୫ ନମ୍ବର)

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

A carefully chosen profession gives a pupil useful work to pursue. A healthy body, supported by regular habits and recreation, helps the pupil carry out that work. Character determines how the pupil deals with others while doing it. Truthfulness, fairness and kindness make personal success valuable to family and nation. The speaker therefore gives advice about work and conduct together. ତିନୋଟି ଦିଗର ପରସ୍ପର ସମ୍ପର୍କ ଦର୍ଶାନ୍ତୁ।

୮. Why is an interesting future mentioned when the speaker discusses jobs?

ଆଦର୍ଶ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନପତ୍ର · ସେଟ୍ ୫, ଭାଗ ୨, ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ ୨ (i) · ବିକଳ୍ପ ୧ (୨.୫ ନମ୍ବର)

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

An immediate job may be temporary or offer little chance to progress. The speaker wants pupils to look for work that can remain worth doing and learning over time.
