A–B · Listening. What changes when the speaker moves from the first three stanzas to the last?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Hear the recurring fall at the end of the first three stanzas. Read the last ending more firmly: the soul is now said to live. Days, flowers and seasons pass; moral goodness has lasting value in the poem. ଏକ ଭାବର ପୁନରାବୃତ୍ତି ଓ ଶେଷରେ ତାହାର ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନକୁ ଉଚ୍ଚାରଣରେ ଧର।

ପ୍ରଥମ ସ୍ତବକଗୁଡ଼ିକର ଶେଷରେ ସ୍ୱରକୁ ଅବସାନ ଦିଅ। ଶେଷ ସ୍ତବକର ଜୀବନସୂଚକ ଶେଷ ସହ ଏହା ତୁଳନା ପାଏ।

ଏହି ଶୁଣାଯାଉଥିବା ବଦଳ କବିତାର ଯୁକ୍ତି।

G. 1–4. Explain the images of the day and the rose.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. The day is cool, calm and bright. 2. Earth and sky are pictured as a bridal pair. 3. Dew seems to weep for the day’s end. 4. The rose is vividly coloured, but rooted in the earth that will be its grave.

Dew ଶାରୀରିକ ଭାବେ କାନ୍ଦୁନାହିଁ। ଶିଶିର ବିନ୍ଦୁ ଲୁହ ପରି ଦେଖାଯିବାରୁ ଦିନର ବିଦାୟର ଭାବ ଆସେ।

Roseର ଆକର୍ଷଣୀୟ ରଙ୍ଗକୁ ତାହାର ମୂଳ ଓ ଶେଷ ସହ ପଢ଼।

G. 5–8. What sets the soul apart?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

5. Spring contains fair days and roses. 6. The soul is sweet and virtuous. 7. It is compared to seasoned timber. 8. Its virtue endures when earthly beauty passes.

Spring ଭିତରେ day ଓ rose ଉଭୟ ଆସନ୍ତି, ତଥାପି ସେହି ଋତୁ ମଧ୍ୟ ଯାଏ। ଗୋଟିଏ, ଅନେକ ଓ ଶେଷରେ virtuous soul ରଖି କବି କେଉଁ ସୌନ୍ଦର୍ଯ୍ୟ ଟିକେ ତାହା କହନ୍ତି।

ମୂଳ ପୁସ୍ତକରେ ପଢ଼ନ୍ତୁ · PDF ପୃଷ୍ଠା ୮୦

H. 1–5. Identify the natural images, personification, colour, root and spring comparison.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. Day, earth, sky, dew and night form the scene. 2. Earth and sky are imagined as a bridal pair, and dew is given the human act of weeping. 3. The rose’s intense colour appears bold and almost challenges an unwary observer. 4. Its root lies in the earth, already its grave. 5. Spring is likened to a box packed with sweets: its lovely days and roses are gathered together. ପ୍ରଥମ ସ୍ତବକର ଦୃଶ୍ୟରେ ମାନବୀୟ କ୍ରିୟା ଆରୋପ ହୋଇଛି।

Bridal pairରେ earth ଓ sky ନିକଟତା ପାଆନ୍ତି, ଶିଶିରରେ ମଣିଷର ଶୋକ ମିଳେ। Root ମାଟିରେ ଥିବାରୁ ସେହି ମାଟିକୁ ଶେଷ ବାସସ୍ଥାନ ଭାବେ ଦେଖିବାର ଚିତ୍ର ହୁଏ।

H. 6–9. Explain music, the enduring soul and the forms of physical beauty.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

6. The speaker’s music is his song, whose cadence closes as spring must end. 7. After three examples of passing beauty he compares a virtuous soul to durable seasoned timber; even a world reduced to coal cannot end its life. 8. The lovely day, rose and spring represent physical beauty. 9. The soul is sweet and virtuous and resists destruction. ତିନିଟି ଅସ୍ଥାୟୀ ଉଦାହରଣ ପରେ ଶେଷ ସ୍ତବକର ତୁଳନାର ପ୍ରଭାବ ବଢ଼େ।

Cadence ଗୀତର ଶେଷ ଧ୍ୱନିକ୍ରମ; springର ଅବସାନ ସହ ସେହି ଶେଷକୁ ଯୋଡ଼ାଯାଏ। ଶାରୀରିକ ରଙ୍ଗ ଓ ରୂପ ସହ ଆଚରଣର ଭଲପଣର ସମୟସୀମା ଭିନ୍ନ।

I (a), (b). List the rhymes and decide whether the seven statements fit the poem.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

a. bright/night, sky/die; brave/grave, eye/die; roses/closes, lie/die; soul/coal, gives/lives. b. 1 False — the sweet day has these qualities. 2 True. 3 True. 4 True. 5 False — a box of sweets. 6 False — the root is already in its grave. 7 True within the poem’s argument. ମତଭେଦ ହେଲେ ସମ୍ପୃକ୍ତ ସ୍ତବକର ଶବ୍ଦ ଦେଖ।

Bright/night ଓ soul/coalରେ ଶେଷ ଶୁଣାଯାଉଥିବା ଭାଗ ମିଳେ। True/Falseରେ ପ୍ରତି statement କେଉଁ stanzaକୁ ନେଇ କହୁଛି ଦେଖ।

ଦିନ ବିଷୟରେ ସତ କଥାଟି soul ବିଷୟରେ ଭୁଲ୍ ହୋଇପାରେ।

J (a). 1–3. Write about the theme, the image of spring and the virtuous soul.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

1. Natural beauty makes the speaker glad, but each example must pass. Virtue gives the soul endurance. 2. Spring contains the lovely days and roses like sweets gathered into one box. Even such abundance will come to a close. 3. A good soul is compared to seasoned timber that holds firm under pressure. Its goodness endures even the imagined destruction of the world. ଉତ୍ତରରେ ଉପମା ସହ ତାହାର ଅର୍ଥ ଲେଖ।

ପ୍ରଥମ ଉତ୍ତର କବିତାର ସାରା ଯୁକ୍ତି, ଦ୍ୱିତୀୟଟି springର ଏକ ଚିତ୍ର, ତୃତୀୟଟି ଉପମାର ଅର୍ଥ ଚାହେ। ପ୍ରତିଥର ସମାନ ସାର ଲେଖିଲେ image ପ୍ରଶ୍ନର ସୂକ୍ଷ୍ମତା ହଜିଯାଏ।

J (b) · Group poem. Brainstorm wishes, choose one and draft a poem beginning with I wish.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Possible wishes: to be patient, to listen carefully, to help a friend. I choose patience because a hasty reply can hurt. Model: I wish my words could pause a while / Before they turn away a smile. / I wish my ears would hear a friend / And listen kindly to the end. ଦଳ ନିଜ ଇଚ୍ଛା ଓ କାରଣ ବାଛି ନିଜ କବିତା ଲେଖିବ।

“I wish to listen before I speak” କହିଲେ patienceର ଦୃଶ୍ୟ ମିଳେ। ପର ପଙ୍କ୍ତିରେ ଏହି ଅଭ୍ୟାସ ସାଙ୍ଗକୁ କିପରି ସହାୟ ହେବ କହ।

ତେବେ ଇଚ୍ଛାରୁ ଭଲ ଆଚରଣର ଫଳକୁ କବିତା ଯିବ।
