A–C · Quotation marks in examples 1–4. How are the spoken words punctuated in the four opening examples, and what replaces that punctuation in the reports?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

The woman’s statement begins with a capital inside quotation marks and ends with a full stop. The order to take Mr Gupta upstairs has a comma before the reporting clause. Sukumar’s question ends with a question mark and his wish with an exclamation mark. The reported versions remove quotation marks and report the senses with that, to, if and that respectively. ୩ରେ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନଚିହ୍ନ ଓ ୪ରେ ବିସ୍ମୟଚିହ୍ନରୁ ମୂଳ କଥାର ଭାବ ଜଣାଯାଏ।

ଉଭୟ ପରୋକ୍ଷ ରୂପ ଏଠାରେ ବିବୃତି ଭାବେ ଶେଷ ହୁଏ।

Statement, question ଓ order ପ୍ରତ୍ୟକ୍ଷରେ ମୂଳ speakerଙ୍କ ଶବ୍ଦ ଓ punctuation ଧରେ। Indirectରେ କଥକ ସୂଚନାକୁ ନିଜ sentenceରେ ଦିଅନ୍ତି।

Order ପାଇଁ told + person + to, question ପାଇଁ asked + clause ଦେଖ।

Before Activity 3 · Time, place, modals and pronouns. In the comparison tables, what changes if the report is made later, elsewhere, or from another speaker’s perspective?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Time examples include now → then, today → that day, yesterday → the previous day and tomorrow → the next day. Here can become there. Will generally becomes would, can becomes could and may becomes might. Must can stay must or become had to or would have to according to meaning. This week becomes that week and these days becomes those days. I/my and you/your must refer to the correct person in the report. ପ୍ରଥମେ କିଏ କାହାକୁ, କେବେ ଓ କେଉଁଠି କଥା କହିଥିଲେ ଚିହ୍ନଟ କର।

ସ୍ଥାନ ଓ ସମୟ ଏକ ରହିଲେ ତାହା ସହ ମେଳ ଖାଉଥିବା ଶବ୍ଦ ରହିପାରେ।

Time/place ଶବ୍ଦ କାହାର ‘ଏବେ’ ଓ ‘ଏଠି’ ଧରିଛି ଦେଖ। ଘଟଣା ସେହିଠି ସେହି ଦିନ report ହେଲେ here/today ରହିପାରେ।

ଜାଗା ଓ ଦିନ ବଦଳିଲେ there/that day ସଠିକ୍ ହୁଏ।

K · Mark the modals, v–ix. In the party, journey, roof, sweet and telephone examples, how is the speaker’s modal reported?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

v must stays must in the proposed celebration. vi must go becomes had to go for necessity. vii must mend becomes would have to mend for a future obligation. viii can I have …?

is a request for permission: the child asked if he could have a sweet, or asked to have one. ix could I speak …? keeps could in the polite telephone request. ଅନୁମତି ମାଗିବା, ଆବଶ୍ୟକତା ଓ ଭବିଷ୍ୟତର କର୍ତ୍ତବ୍ୟକୁ ଏକା ଭାବେ ଅନୁବାଦ କରିବ ନାହିଁ।

Mustର ଅର୍ଥ ପ୍ରସ୍ତାବ, ବାଧ୍ୟତା ବା ଭବିଷ୍ୟତ ଆବଶ୍ୟକତା ହୋଇପାରେ। Journey ପାଇଁ ପୂର୍ବ ଆବଶ୍ୟକତା had to; roofର ପରର କାମ would have to।

L · Exclamations and modals, i–x. What reporting expressions convey the eight exclamations or greetings? How are the two can-questions and replies reported?

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

i exclaimed in joy, ii exclaimed in sorrow, iii wished me good luck, iv congratulated me, v called me a liar, vi exclaimed that it was very dreadful, vii greeted me and asked where I lived, viii wished me a good morning. In ix, can → could and No → I could not. In x, can → could and Yes → I could. Reporting the circus question later changes this evening to that evening. ବିସ୍ମୟଚିହ୍ନ ବଦଳରେ ଅର୍ଥ ଅନୁଯାୟୀ ଆନନ୍ଦ, ଦୁଃଖ, ଅଭିନନ୍ଦନ ବା ଶୁଭେଚ୍ଛାର reporting verb ବାଛ।

Good luck କହିଲେ wished, successର ପ୍ରତିକ୍ରିୟା congratulated, liar କହିଲେ called me a liar। Can you …?

ପ୍ରକୃତ କ୍ଷମତା ପଚାରୁଛି ନା ନିବେଦନ କରୁଛି ତାହା ମୂଳ replyରୁ ଚିହ୍ନ।

Activity 7 (i–iv). Report the advice, warning and two-part remark.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

i. He advised me to consult a doctor immediately. ii. He urged me to hurry and warned that otherwise I would miss the train. iii. He advised me not to get addicted to tea. iv. He said my mouth smelt foul and told me not to open it. (iv)ରେ ଶ୍ରୋତା ନିଜେ ରିପୋର୍ଟ କରୁଛନ୍ତି ବୋଲି ଧରାଯାଇଛି।

Don’t ଥିବା ଆଦେଶରେ not to + verb।

v. The captain exclaimed with joy that they had scored two goals. vi. The General applauded them and said that they had done wonders. vii. The teacher exclaimed that Nirmal was a very lazy boy. viii. My friend said that it was very kind of me to have come. Hurrah ଆନନ୍ଦ ଓ Bravo ପ୍ରଶଂସା ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

Activity 7 (ix–xi). Report the farewell and the two wishes.

ix. The traveller bade his friends farewell. x. The beggar longed for a morsel of food. xi. He wished he had the wings of a dove. ଶେଷ ବାକ୍ୟରେ କଳ୍ପିତ ଇଚ୍ଛା ପାଇଁ wished ଓ had ରହିବ। ବିଦାୟ ଓ କିଛି ପାଇବାର ଆକାଙ୍କ୍ଷା ଭିନ୍ନ ଭାବ।

Activity 8. Report Maggie’s conversation, including her final request.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

Maggie asked me whether I was leaving that day. I replied that I was and added that it was the day of my departure. She asked how long it would take to reach my country and which part of it I lived in. I replied that I lived in Punjab, in the west. With tears in her eyes, she asked me to put a flower on her brother’s grave. ଯାତ୍ରୀ ସ୍ଥାନର ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଇଥିଲେ।

ସମୟ ସମ୍ପର୍କିତ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନର ଉତ୍ତର ଦିଆଯାଇନଥିବାରୁ ନୂଆ ତଥ୍ୟ ଯୋଡ଼ାଯାଇନାହିଁ।

ଏକାଧିକ ପାଳିରେ ପ୍ରତିଥର କିଏ କହୁଛି ଚିହ୍ନ। Maggie ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ କଲେ she asked, ମୁଁ ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଲେ I replied।
