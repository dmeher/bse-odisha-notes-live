After Activity 2 · Conditional examples 3a–d. Compare the positions of the conditions, punctuation and verb forms in the four model pairs.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

3a if + present, present gives a regular result. 3b if + present, will + verb gives a possible future result. In these pairs the initial if-clause takes a comma, while a final if-clause follows without one. 3c if + past, would + verb imagines an unreal situation. 3d if + past perfect, could have + past participle imagines a missed past possibility. In 3c–d the alternative openings Were wishes … and Had he arrived … omit if and keep the introductory comma. ସର୍ତ୍ତ ଆଗରେ ନା ପଛରେ ଅଛି ଦେଖ।

Were ଓ Had ଆଗକୁ ଆସିଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ ଏହି ଦୁଇ ବାକ୍ୟର ସର୍ତ୍ତର ଅର୍ଥ ରହେ।

Condition ପ୍ରଥମେ ଥିଲେ ପୂରା if-clause ପରେ comma। Main clause ଆଗେ ଥିଲେ ସାଧାରଣତଃ ସେହି comma ଲାଗେନାହିଁ।

Present/present ନିୟମିତ ଫଳ; present/will ସମ୍ଭାବ୍ୟ ଫଳ; past/would କଳ୍ପିତ; had + V3/would have + V3 ଅତୀତର ଅଘଟିତ ଫଳ।

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବୁଝାମଣା — ଏକା ଅର୍ଥକୁ because ସହ ଗୋଟିଏ dependent clause ବା so ସହ ଦୁଇଟି ସ୍ୱାଧୀନ ଅଂଶରେ ଲେଖାଯାଇପାରେ।

Because/as ଥିବା ଅଂଶକୁ ଏକାକୀ ରଖିଲେ ବାକ୍ୟ ଅଧା ଲାଗେ। So ପୂର୍ବରେ କାରଣଟି ନିଜେ ଏକ ବାକ୍ୟ ହୋଇପାରେ।

Activity 8. Identify the subordinate clause in the conversation about buying milk.

ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ

If they have eggs is an adverb clause of condition. It qualifies get six. Tuna understands six to mean six packets of milk, although Tuni has just mentioned eggs. The question about buying milk is direct speech and is an independent question. ଅଣ୍ଡା ମିଳିବା ସର୍ତ୍ତ ବୁଝାପଡ଼ିଛି।

ଭୁଲ ବୁଝାମଣା ହୋଇଛି six ପରେ କେଉଁ ନାମପଦ ଉହ୍ୟ ଅଛି ତାହାକୁ ନେଇ।

If they have eggs ଅଂଶଟି ନ ଘଟିଲେ ‘get six’ ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦେଶ ଲାଗୁ ହୁଏନାହିଁ। ମଜାର ମୂଳ ସର୍ତ୍ତରେ ନୁହେଁ, six ପରେ କେଉଁ noun ନ କୁହାଯାଇଛି ସେଥିରେ: eggs ନା packets of milk?
