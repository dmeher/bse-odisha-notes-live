    2. Indirect object
    3. Direct object

    1. are
    2. is
    3. has
    4. was

    1. have
    2. are
    3. were
    4. has

  37. Choose the correct completion: She would not let us ___ the letter.

    1. reading
    2. reads
    3. read
    4. to read

  38. Which fits “The pupils hope ___ their teacher soon”?

    1. sees
    2. to see
    3. seeing
    4. saw

  39. The train leaves ___ midnight.

    1. at
    2. on
    3. in
    4. for

  40. She took the pencil ___ her pocket.

    1. across
    2. over
    3. along
    4. out of

  41. The organisers called off the match. What did they do?

    1. Recorded it
    2. Repeated it
    3. Cancelled it
    4. Finished playing it

  42. A pupil who can keep up with the class can ___.

    1. call off a meeting
    2. work at the same pace
    3. leave it forever
    4. teach every lesson

  43. Identify the function of “what I say” after “pay attention to”.

    1. Object of a preposition
    2. Subject of pay
    3. Relative clause modifying attention
    4. Adverb of time

  44. Extra information about a named person is usually enclosed by ___.

    1. question marks
    2. brackets only
    3. exclamation marks
    4. commas

  45. “I stayed home because I was busy.” The clause gives a ___.

    1. condition
    2. purpose
    3. reason
    4. place

  46. If ice is heated, it ___.

    1. were melting
    2. melts
    3. had melted yesterday
    4. would have melted then

  47. The walls ___ when I visited yesterday.

    1. were being painted
    2. are painting
    3. have painted
    4. have been painting

  48. What is the passive of “They must finish the work”?

    1. The work must finished.
    2. The work must have finish.
    3. The work is must finish.
    4. The work must be finished.

  49. He says, “I am tired.” Choose the report.

    1. He says that he has tired.
    2. He says that is he tired.
    3. He says that he is tired.
    4. He says that I am tired.

  50. Which reports a question without retaining question order?

    1. She asked that arrived the train.
    2. She asked if the train had arrived.
    3. She asked had the train arrived?
    4. She asked if had the train arrived.
